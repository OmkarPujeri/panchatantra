The Old Man with the Young Wife

 

The old merchant's name was Lovelorn. He married a young girl. She was unhappy. She did not love him.

The old man was weak. He could not work much. His wife did not like him. She walked away from him. She did not want to live with him.

One night, a thief came to their house. The young girl was scared. She hugged her husband. The old man was surprised. He thought, "Why is she hugging me?"

The old man saw the thief. He thought, "Maybe she is hugging me because she is scared of him." He said to the thief, "Thank you! You can take what you want."

The thief said, "I don't want anything. If I ever want something, I will come back when she does not hug you."

Foe-Crusher, a king, heard about this story. His counselors said the guest should not be killed. Help may come from a benefactor, even a thief.

Moral: Help from unexpected people can be very useful.
