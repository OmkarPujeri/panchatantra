The Unteachable Monkey

A group of monkeys lived in a forest.

They were very cold one winter evening. 
They saw a firefly and thought it was fire. 
They covered it with dry grass and leaves. 
They put their arms around it and felt warm. 
One monkey, Red-Face, was very cold. 
He blew on the firefly to make it warmer.

A bird named Needle-Face flew down. 
She said, "This is not fire, it's a firefly." 
Red-Face didn't listen. He blew again. 
Needle-Face tried to stop him, but he didn't listen. 
She shouted in his ear, "Stop! It's not fire!" 
Red-Face got angry and grabbed Needle-Face. 
He threw her on a rock, and she died.

Moral: Don't be stubborn and listen to others.
