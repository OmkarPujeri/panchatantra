Ill-considered Action

A merchant named Jewel lived in Trumpet-Flower city.

He lost his fortune. His life was full of worries.

He thought, "A curse on this poverty! I will starve myself to death."

A Jain monk appeared to him in a dream. "I am a trillion dollars. Club me on the head and I will turn to gold."

Jewel woke up and thought, "This dream will not come true. I think of money all the time."

A barber came to manicure his wife's nails. The Jain monk appeared. Jewel was happy and hit the monk with a stick.

The monk turned to gold. Jewel put him in the house.

The barber thought, "I will invite many monks and club them to get gold."

The barber went to a monastery and asked for monks. He clubbed them, but they did not turn to gold.

The soldiers heard the noise and came. They saw the monks running with blood.

The barber was caught and taken to court. The judges asked him, "Why did you kill the monks?"

The barber said, "I was doing what the merchant told me to do."

The judges sent for the merchant. He told the story of the monk.

The judges said, "The barber's act was wrong. He should be punished."

Moral: Think before you act, or you may bring trouble on yourself.
