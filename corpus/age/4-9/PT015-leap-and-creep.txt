Leap and Creep

In a king's palace, a bed was very special.
It was soft and clean, with a sweet smell.
A louse named Creep lived on the bed.
She had many children and grandchildren.
She ate the king's blood while he slept.

One day, a flea named Leap came to the bed.
He was very happy to be there.
He said, "This bed is wonderful! I'm so lucky!"
Creep said, "You shouldn't be here. Leave now!"
Leap said, "But I'm a guest. I want to stay."
He said, "I've tasted many kinds of blood, but yours is the best."

Creep didn't want Leap to eat the king's blood.
She said, "You're too big and strong. You might hurt the king."
Leap fell on his knees and asked again.
Creep said, "Okay, but you must wait until the king is asleep."

Leap agreed, but he bit the king on the back.
The king woke up and scratched his back.
He shouted, "Someone bit me! Find the insect!"
Leap was scared and ran into a small hole.

The king's servants came with a lamp and looked around.
They found Creep and her family in the bed.
They killed them all.

Moral: Don't invite strangers to stay in your home.
