The Farmer's Wife

There was a farmer who lived with his wife.

The farmer's wife was not happy at home. She thought about strange men all the time.

A rogue saw her and said, "You are very beautiful. My wife is dead. I want to be with you."

The farmer's wife said, "My husband has a lot of money. He is old and cannot work. I will bring the money, and we can go to a city together."

The rogue said, "That's a good idea. Meet me here at dawn, and we will go to the city."

The farmer's wife went home with a happy face. At night, she took all the money and met the rogue at dawn.

The rogue said, "This is a big river. I will put the money on the other side and come back for you."

But the rogue took the money and left the farmer's wife alone on the riverbank.

A she-jackal came to the riverbank with some meat. A fish jumped out of the water and was stuck on the bank. The she-jackal ran after the fish, but a vulture took the meat. The fish went back into the water.

The she-jackal looked at the farmer's wife and said, "You are as unlucky as I am. Your lover took your money, and now you are naked."

The farmer's wife smiled and said, "You are right. I am as unlucky as you are. My lover took my money and left me."

Just then, a crocodile told a story about a farmer's wife who was unlucky. A water-beast came and said, "The crocodile's house has been taken over by another crocodile."

The crocodile was sad and thought about what to do. He asked his monkey friend for advice.

The monkey said, "You are foolish. I will not give you advice."

But at last the monkey helped him. He told the crocodile to be brave and fight for his home.

The crocodile went home. He fought the other crocodile and won. He got his house back and lived there happily.

Moral: Bad things often happen together.
