The Gullible Carpenter

There was a carpenter in a village.

He wanted to test his wife's faithfulness. He said to her, "Tomorrow, I will go to another village. Please make me a lunch."

His wife was happy to hear this. She made delicious food. She spent the day trying on her best clothes.

She went to her old lover and said, "My husband is away. Please come to our house at night."

The lover came to the house. The carpenter hid under the bed. He saw his wife's lover getting into bed.

The carpenter was angry. But he hid and waited. His wife went to bed and locked the door.

She stubbed her toe on the carpenter's body. She thought it was the carpenter testing her. She said to the lover, "I invited you to help my husband live a hundred years."

The lover asked why. She said, "The goddess said my husband would die soon. If I hugged another man, the death would pass to him. Then my husband would live a hundred years. So I invited you."

The lover did as she said. The carpenter came out from under the bed and hugged his wife. He said, "You are a faithful wife. I pretended to go away to test you."

The lover was forced to mount the carpenter's shoulder. The carpenter went dancing to the doors of his relatives' houses.

Moral: It is foolish to forgive someone who has done you wrong.
