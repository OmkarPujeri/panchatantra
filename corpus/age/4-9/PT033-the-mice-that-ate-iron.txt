The Mice That Ate Iron

There was a merchant named Naduk.
He lost his money and went abroad.
He spent his money like a gentleman.
Now, he returned to his home town.

Naduk had an iron balance-beam.
It weighed a thousand pounds.
He gave it to Merchant Lakshman.
He wanted it back, but it was gone.

Lakshman said, "Mice ate the balance-beam."
Naduk said, "It's okay. Such is life."
He asked Lakshman to send his son Money-God.
To carry his bathing things to the river.

Lakshman's son went with Naduk to the river.
Naduk took a bath and then did something bad.
He put the son in a mountain cave.
Blocked the entrance with a big rock.

When Lakshman asked for his son, Naduk lied.
He said a hawk carried him away.
Lakshman was angry and took it to the palace.
The magistrates said, "Naduk, return the boy!"

Naduk lied again, but then he told the truth.
Mice can eat iron, and a hawk can steal a big boy.
Everyone laughed, and the balance-beam and boy were returned.

Moral: Honesty is always the best choice.
