Slow, the Weaver

In a town, a weaver named Slow lived. 
He had a loom but all pegs broke.

Slow went to the seashore to find wood. 
There, he saw a big sissoo tree.

A fairy lived in the tree. "Please spare it," the fairy said. 
"My home is here. I'm happy here."

"Why not move?" Slow asked. "I need wood for my loom." 
The fairy said, "Ask me anything, but spare this tree."

Slow went home to ask his friend and wife. 
At home, he met his friend, the barber.

"My friend, the fairy favors me," Slow said. 
"Ask for a kingdom. We'll be king and prime minister."

Slow's wife was not at home. He asked his friend about her. 
The friend said, "Don't ask her. Women give bad advice."

Slow went to his wife. "What to ask the fairy?" he asked. 
She said, "Don't be silly. Ask for a second pair of arms and an extra head."

"I can make two pieces of cloth," Slow thought. 
"I'll be happy and rich."

Slow went back to the fairy. "I want a second pair of arms and an extra head," he said. 
The fairy gave him what he asked for.

But people saw Slow and thought he was a monster. 
They beat him with sticks and stones.

Slow died.

Moral: Ask for what you really need, not what looks nice.
