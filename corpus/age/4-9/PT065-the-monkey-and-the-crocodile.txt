The Monkey and the Crocodile

Two friends, Red-Face the monkey and Ugly-Mug the crocodile, lived under a rose-apple tree.

Red-Face gave Ugly-Mug rose-apples to eat. They talked happily every day.

Ugly-Mug went home and gave his wife the rose-apples. She asked where he got them. He said a monkey friend gave them to him.

His wife said, "If the fruit is so sweet, the monkey's heart must be sweet too. Ask him to give us his heart. We will eat it and never be old or sick."

Ugly-Mug said, "He is my adopted brother. I cannot kill him."

His wife said, "You love him, that's why you won't give us his heart. You sigh and kiss me but don't hug me tight. I know you love another woman."

Ugly-Mug was sad. He said, "Why do you look at me with jealousy?"

His wife cried and said, "You love her, you deceiver. Your heart belongs to her. My rivalry is useless."

Ugly-Mug was worried. He thought, "What shall I do? How can I kill the monkey?" He visited the monkey.

The monkey asked, "Why are you sad? Why don't you speak cheerfully?"

Ugly-Mug said, "My wife scolded me. She said I live at your expense and don't show you any gratitude. She wants you to come home with me."

The monkey said, "Your home is in the water. How can I go there? Bring your wife here instead, that I may bow down and receive her blessing."

Ugly-Mug said, "Climb on my back, and I will take you home."

The monkey sat on Ugly-Mug's back. When they were in the ocean, the monkey was scared. He said, "Go slow, brother. The waves are too strong."

Ugly-Mug thought, "If he falls, he can't move. He is in my power. I will tell him my plan."

He said, "Sir, I deceived you. My wife bade me kill you. So pray to your favorite god."

The monkey said, "What harm have I done you or your wife? Why do you want to kill me?"

Ugly-Mug said, "Those rose-apples were too sweet. My wife wants to eat your heart."

The monkey said, "Why didn't you tell me on shore? I have another heart, sweet and nice, in the rose-apple tree. Give me that one instead."

Ugly-Mug was happy. He said, "If you have another heart, give it to me. My wife will eat it, and she will never be old or sick."

The monkey laughed and said, "You fool! You traitor! How can anyone have two hearts? Go home, and never come back under the rose-apple tree."

Moral: Whoever trusts a faithless friend will find death.
