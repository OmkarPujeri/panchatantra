The Plover Who Fought the Ocean

 

A plover and his wife lived by the sea. They were called Sprawl and Constance.

The sea had many fish, crocodiles, and turtles. Constance was pregnant and wanted to lay her eggs safely.

Sprawl said, "Don't worry, this is our home. The ocean is far away." Constance said, "But the ocean is close. Its waves might hurt my babies."

Sprawl said, "The ocean won't hurt me. I'm a strong plover." He started talking loudly and said, "Who dares to fight a snake? Who touches a hot fire?"

Constance laughed and said, "You talk big, but you're not brave. You're just a loud talker." She said, "To know yourself is hard. You should think before acting."

Constance told a story about a turtle who listened to his friends. He was happy and safe. Sprawl asked, "How was that?" Constance said, "It's wise to listen to friends."

Constance laid her eggs on the shore. Sprawl was proud and sure the ocean would leave them alone. But the ocean had heard his big talk. One day a wave reached up and stole the eggs. Constance was very sad. Sprawl said, "I will dry up the ocean with my beak!" Constance said, "You need help. Call all the birds." So the birds came. They beat the water with their wings and dropped dust into it. But the ocean was too big. An old gander said, "Our king is Garuda. Ask him for help." The birds cried out to Garuda. Garuda served the great god Vishnu. He told Vishnu what the ocean had done. Vishnu came and spoke to the ocean. "Give back the eggs," he said. The ocean was afraid. It gave the eggs back to the plover. Sprawl and Constance were glad.

Moral: Listen to others and think before acting.
