Numskull and the Rabbit

Numskull was a lion. He was very proud.

He would kill any animal he saw.

All the animals in the forest were afraid of him.

They went to Numskull and said, "Please stop killing us. It's not right."

Numskull said, "I will stop killing you if you send me one animal every day for food."

The animals agreed. Each day, one animal would come to Numskull.

One day, it was a rabbit's turn. The rabbit came late, and Numskull was angry.

The rabbit said, "Master, I'm sorry I'm late. But I had to come and tell you something."

Numskull said, "What is it? Speak quickly."

The rabbit said, "A lion came out of a hole in the ground and said I had to bring you to him. He said you were a sneak thief."

Numskull said, "Show me that lion. I will kill him."

The rabbit said, "Follow me, master." He led Numskull to a well.

Numskull looked into the well and saw his own reflection. He thought it was the lion and roared loudly.

The rabbit said, "Look, master. Your rival is very powerful."

Numskull jumped into the well and drowned.

The rabbit was happy and told all the animals the good news.

Moral: Intelligence is power.
