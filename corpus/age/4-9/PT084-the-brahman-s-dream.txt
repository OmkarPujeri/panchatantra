The Brahman's Dream

There lived a Brahman named Seedy in a town.
He begged for barley-meal and got some.
He ate a little and filled a jar with the rest.
He hung the jar on a peg and went to sleep.

Seedy thought, "If famine comes, I'll get 100 rupees from this jar."
He would buy she-goats, and they would have babies.
More goats, cows, buffaloes, and mares would come.
He would sell them and get rich.
He would buy a big house and a lovely daughter.
She would have a son named Moon-Lord.

Seedy imagined himself sitting on the stable roof.
Moon-Lord would jump from his mother's lap to ride on Seedy's knee.
But Seedy would get angry and tell his wife to take the boy.
She would be busy and not listen.

Seedy kicked the jar in his dream.
It broke, and barley-meal spilled all over him.
Seedy turned white from head to toe.

Moral: Don't let your dreams make you forget what's real.
