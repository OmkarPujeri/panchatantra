Hundred-Wit, Thousand-Wit, and Single-Wit

Three friends lived in a pond. They were Hundred-Wit, Thousand-Wit, and Single-Wit.

They loved to talk and play together.

One day, some fishermen came to the pond. They said, "This pond has many fish. We will come back tomorrow."

The three friends were very scared. They talked about what to do.

Single-Wit said, "Let's leave the pond and go somewhere else."

Thousand-Wit said, "Don't worry. I'm very clever. I can save us."

Hundred-Wit said, "I'm clever too. I can save us."

But Single-Wit was not convinced. He said, "I'm going to leave."

He and his wife left the pond at night.

The next morning, the fishermen came with nets. They caught all the fish, including Hundred-Wit and Thousand-Wit.

But Single-Wit was safe in a cistern. He said to his wife, "Look, I'm safe!"

The fishermen took Hundred-Wit and Thousand-Wit away. Single-Wit was happy to be safe.

Moral: Being clever is not enough to save you. Sometimes, common sense and quick thinking can be more important.
