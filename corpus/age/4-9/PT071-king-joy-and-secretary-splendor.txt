King Joy and Secretary Splendor

There was a king named Joy. He was a powerful king.

He had a secretary named Splendor. Splendor was very smart.

Splendor's wife was angry with him. She said, "Shave your head and fall at my feet."

Splendor did what she said. His wife forgave him.

King Joy's wife was angry with him too. She said, "Hold a bit in your mouth and let me ride on your back. Make horse noises when I drive you."

King Joy did what she said. His wife forgave him.

The next morning, Splendor came to King Joy. King Joy asked, "Why is your head shaved?"

Splendor said, "I shaved my head to make my wife happy."

King Joy laughed and said, "You are just like me! My wife asked me to do silly things too."

Splendor said, "Yes, I tried to make my wife happy. But I said something that gave me away."

King Joy asked, "What did you say?"

Splendor said, "I said I would do what my wife asked. But I didn't mean it."

Moral: Be careful what you say, it can get you into trouble.
