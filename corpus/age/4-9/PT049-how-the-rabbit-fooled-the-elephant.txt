How the Rabbit Fooled the Elephant

There was a big elephant named Four-Tusk. He had many elephants with him.

There was a big drought. The elephants were very thirsty. They asked Four-Tusk to find water.

Four-Tusk sent his elephants to find water. They found a beautiful lake called Lake of the Moon. It was full of water, fish, and birds.

The elephants were very happy. They went to the lake and played in the water. But they didn't see the rabbits who lived there.

The rabbits were very sad. They held a meeting to think of a plan. A rabbit named Victory said, "Don't worry, I have a plan."

Victory said, "I will go to Four-Tusk and tell him that I am an envoy of the Moon. He will listen to me and do what I say."

Victory went to Four-Tusk and said, "I am an envoy of the Moon. I have come to tell you that you have done something wrong."

Four-Tusk asked, "What have I done wrong?" Victory said, "You went to the Lake of the Moon and hurt the rabbits who live there."

Four-Tusk said, "I didn't know it was wrong." Victory said, "You should have known. The Moon is very angry with you."

Victory told Four-Tusk to go to the lake and touch the water. Four-Tusk did what Victory said. But then the Moon got even angrier.

Victory said, "Now the Moon is very angry with you. You should go back to your home and never come back to the lake."

Four-Tusk was very sorry. He went back to his home and never came back to the lake.

Moral: Sometimes, being sorry is not enough. You must also make things right.
