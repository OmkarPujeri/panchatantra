The Monk Who Left His Body Behind

A king named Fine-Chariot ruled Unassailable city.

He received a report from a forest ranger. The forest kings were being very bad. One of them was named Vindhyaka. The king sent Counselor Strong to teach them good manners.

A naked monk came to the city. He was very smart about stars and planets. The people liked him very much.

The king invited the monk to the palace. The monk said he could read people's thoughts. The king was very curious.

The monk didn't come to the palace one day. The next day, he said, "I went to heaven and talked to the gods. They asked me about you, King."

The king believed the monk. He forgot about his duties and spent all his time with the monk.

Counselor Strong came back from the forest. He found the king being silly. Strong said, "King, you are being foolish!"

The king asked Strong about the monk. Strong said, "I know the monk is very smart. I heard he goes to heaven every day."

The monk said, "If you want to know, you can go see for yourself." The monk went into his cell and locked the door.

Strong said, "King, let's burn the monk's body. Then he will come back in his other body." The king agreed.

Strong brought firewood and set the cell on fire. The monk didn't come back. He was just pretending.

Moral: Don't believe everything you hear, even if someone says it's true.
