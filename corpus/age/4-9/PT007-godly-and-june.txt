Godly and June

There was a holy man named Godly.

He lived in a monastery with many treasures.

People gave him money for his sacrifices.

Godly trusted no one with his treasure.

He kept it under his arm always.

A rogue named June saw the treasure.

June thought, "How can I take this treasure?"

He couldn't break the stone wall.

He couldn't enter the door, it was too high.

June decided to befriend Godly.

He went to Godly and said, "Glory to Shiva."

June fell at Godly's feet and said, "I am a foolish man."

Godly was happy to have a new friend.

June said, "I am worried about the future."

Godly said, "You are wise to think about this."

Godly gave June advice on how to live.

He said, "You can change your life with prayer."

June was impressed and asked for a vow.

Godly agreed, but said June must sleep outside.

June was happy to take the vow.

Godly gave June initiation and made him a disciple.

June served Godly, but still kept an eye on the treasure.

June thought of killing Godly to get the treasure.

But then a pupil of Godly's came with an invitation.

Godly and June went to the ceremony.

At the river, Godly gave June his robe to watch.

June took the treasure and ran away.

Moral: Trusting a false friend can lead to trouble.
