The Mice That Set Elephants Free

There was a big, old village with broken houses.

Mice lived in the cracks of the walls. They were happy.

They had parties, watched plays, and ate yummy food.

But one day, a big elephant king came with his herd.

He crushed some mice with his big feet.

The mice were sad and scared. They had a meeting.

"We must find a way to stay safe," they said.

Some mice went to the elephant king.

"We live near here," they said. "Please don't hurt us."

The elephant king thought about it. He decided to help the mice.

Years later, the elephant king got trapped.

He remembered the mice. They were his only hope.

He sent a message to the mice through an elephant cow.

The mice came to help their friends. They gnawed the ropes.

The elephant king and his herd were free.

Moral: Make friends with everyone, big or small.
