Forethought, Readywit, and Fatalist

There were three fish friends living in a big lake.

Forethought was very thoughtful. He heard some people talking on the bank. They said, "Tomorrow we will go fishing here."

Forethought thought, "This is not good. They might catch us. I will ask my friends, Readywit and Fatalist, to come with me to a new lake."

Readywit said, "I don't want to move. If the fishermen come, I will hide and protect myself."

Fatalist said, "Why worry? We might not go fishing here. Let's stay in our own lake."

Forethought thought, "Okay, I will go to the new lake by myself." He left his friends.

The next day, the fishermen came with a big net. They caught all the fish in the lake, including Readywit and Fatalist. But Readywit pretended to be dead and escaped. Fatalist got stuck in the net and was beaten to death.

Moral: Think ahead and be prepared, or you might get hurt.
