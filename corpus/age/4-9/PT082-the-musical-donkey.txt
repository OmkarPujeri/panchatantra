The Musical Donkey

The donkey Prig carried laundry packages during the day. At night, he was free to roam.

Prig met a jackal one night in the fields. They became friends. They broke through a hedge into a cucumber bed and ate some cucumbers.

Next night, Prig said to the jackal, "Let's sing a song." The jackal said, "No, it's not a good idea. We might get caught."

The jackal said, "Your singing is loud and will wake up the farmers. They will catch us." Prig said, "My singing is beautiful. I will sing anyway."

The jackal said, "Please don't. It's not safe." Prig said, "I know what I'm doing. I will sing."

The jackal said, "Okay, I will stay here and look for farmers. You can sing." Prig started to sing. The farmers heard him and came running.

They caught Prig and beat him with sticks. They put a heavy mortar on his neck. Prig stood up and forgot about the pain.

The jackal laughed and said, "Well done, Prig! You sang so loudly that the farmers caught us." Prig said, "I'm glad I sang."

The jackal said, "You should have listened to me. Now you're wearing a heavy mortar on your neck." Prig realized his mistake.

Moral: Listen to your friends when they give you good advice.
