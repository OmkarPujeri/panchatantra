The Shrewd Old Gander

In a forest, a big fig tree stood tall.

Under its branches lived many wild geese.

A creeping vine, called koshambi, grew up the tree.

The old gander said, "This vine is bad for us.

Someone might climb up and hurt us.

Please cut it down while it's small."

But the geese didn't listen to the old gander.

The vine grew strong and climbed up the tree.

One day, while the geese were out, a hunter came.

He climbed the tree using the vine and set a trap.

The geese returned home at night, but were caught.

The old gander said, "I told you so.

We should have cut the vine when it was small."

The geese asked the old gander, "What can we do now?"

He said, "When the hunter comes, pretend to be dead.

When he throws us away, we'll all fly up together."

The hunter came and thought the geese were dead.

He freed them from the trap and threw them down.

But when he was about to leave, the geese flew up.

They were all safe because of the old gander's plan.

Moral: Always listen to old people's wise advice.
