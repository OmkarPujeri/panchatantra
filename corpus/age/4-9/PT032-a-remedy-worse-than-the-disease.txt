A Remedy Worse than the Disease

There was a big fig tree in the forest.

Many herons lived in its branches. A black snake lived in a hole in the tree.

The snake ate the heron babies. A heron was very sad.

He went to the pond and cried. A crab saw him. "Why are you crying?" asked the crab.

"My babies and cousins' babies are eaten by a snake," said the heron. "Please help me!" The crab thought for a moment.

"I have an idea," said the crab. "Tell the other herons to put fish bits from the mungoose's home to the snake's hole. The mungoose will follow the fish and kill the snake."

The herons did what the crab said. The mungoose followed the fish and killed the snake. But then it ate all the herons in the tree.

The herons were very sad again. They realized that the crab's plan was not good.

Moral: Be careful what you ask for, it might make things worse.
