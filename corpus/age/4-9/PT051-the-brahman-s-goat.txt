The Brahman's Goat

A Brahman named Friendly lived in a town. He kept a sacred fire.

One day, Friendly went to another village. He needed a goat for a sacrifice. A man gave him a plump goat.

Friendly put the goat on his shoulder and started home. Three hungry rogues saw the goat. They thought, "We can eat this goat."

The first rogue met Friendly. He said, "Brahman, you carry a dog on your shoulder. It's not right."

Friendly got angry. "You're blind! This is a goat, not a dog."

The second rogue met Friendly. He said, "Brahman, you carry a dead calf. It's not good."

Friendly got angry again. "You're blind! This is a goat, not a calf."

The third rogue met Friendly. He said, "Brahman, you carry a donkey. It's not proper."

Friendly got very angry. He threw the goat on the ground and ran home.

The three rogues caught the goat and ate it. They laughed and said, "We fooled the Brahman!"

Moral: Be careful not to listen to people who try to trick you.
