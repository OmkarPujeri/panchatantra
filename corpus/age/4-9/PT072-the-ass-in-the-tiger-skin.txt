The Ass in the Tiger-Skin

A laundryman named Clean-Cloth lived in a town.

He had a very weak and thin donkey. Clean-Cloth took care of the donkey.

One day, while walking in the forest, Clean-Cloth saw a dead tiger. He thought, "This is a good idea."

He put the tiger-skin on the donkey's back. The donkey looked very scary. Clean-Cloth took the donkey to the barley fields at night.

The donkey ate as much barley as he wanted. Clean-Cloth took him back to the barn at dawn.

The donkey grew very plump and fat. He had to squeeze to get into the stall. Clean-Cloth was happy.

But one day, the donkey heard another donkey's loud bray. He started braying too. The farmers heard the donkey's bray and saw that he was wearing a tiger-skin.

They threw stones, clubs, and arrows at the donkey. He got hurt very badly.

The donkey died. Clean-Cloth was very sad.

Moral: Even clever tricks cannot fool everyone forever.
