The Cat's Judgment

There was a partridge. He was a good friend of mine. We spent our evenings together, playing games and sharing stories.

One day, the partridge didn't come home. I missed him and thought he might be in trouble.

A rabbit named Speedy moved into the partridge's old nest. I didn't stop him because I thought the partridge was gone forever.

But the partridge came back one day. He was very plump from eating rice. He saw Speedy in the nest and said, "You're in my home! Please leave!"

Speedy said, "A home is yours only while you occupy it." The partridge said, "Let's ask the neighbors to decide."

The partridge and Speedy went to ask the neighbors, but they couldn't agree. They decided to ask a wise cat named Curd-Ear to judge their dispute.

When they met Curd-Ear, Speedy was scared. He said, "Don't trust him! He's a trickster!" But Curd-Ear pretended to be a holy cat and gave a long speech about the importance of being kind and moral.

The partridge and Speedy were fooled and drew near to Curd-Ear. He quickly ate them both.

Moral: A dishonest judge can cause great harm.
