Shell-Neck, Slim, and Grim

There was a turtle named Shell-Neck.
He lived in a lake with friends Slim and Grim.
They were two ganders.

A big drought came to the land.
The lake dried up. Slim and Grim said, "Goodbye, Shell-Neck. We must leave."

Shell-Neck said, "Please don't go. I will die here. You can fly away, but the water is drying up."

Slim and Grim said, "We can't take you with us. You can't fly."

Shell-Neck said, "Bring a stick. I can grip it with my teeth. You can hold it with your bills and fly with me."

Slim and Grim said, "But if you talk, you will fall."

Shell-Neck said, "I promise to be quiet."

They flew over a city. People looked up and asked, "What are you carrying?"

Shell-Neck said, "What's all the noise about?"

He lost his grip and fell. People cut him up with knives.

Moral: Always listen carefully to good advice.
