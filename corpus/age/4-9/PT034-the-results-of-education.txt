The Results of Education

A hen-parrot had two chicks. The hunter caught them when the mother was away.

One chick escaped. The other was kept in a cage and taught to speak.

The escaped chick met a holy man. He took the chick to his hermitage and cared for it.

The king's horse ran away. He came to the forest where the hunter lived.

The parrot in the cage shouted, "Bind the king! Kill him!" The king ran away.

The king came to another forest. He saw a hermitage and a parrot in a cage.

The parrot said, "Welcome, King! Rest here. Drink cool water and eat sweet fruit."

The king thought, "This parrot is kind. What's the difference between this one and the other?"

He told the parrot about the other parrot who shouted, "Bind him! Kill him!"

The parrot said, "I was raised among holy men. So I am kind. The other parrot was raised among hunters. So he was taught to be cruel."

Moral: Education shows its true results.
