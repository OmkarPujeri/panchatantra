The Unsocial Swans

There was a king named Gay-Chariot.

He owned a lake named Lotus Lake. His soldiers guarded it carefully. Many golden swans lived there. They gave one tail-feather every six months.

A great bird, all gold, came to the lake. The swans said, "You cannot live here. We rent this lake." A dispute arose.

The great bird went to the king. "Those birds are not polite," he said. "I'll tell the king." The king said, "Kill all the birds and bring them here."

The king's men had clubs. One old bird said, "Let's fly away." They all flew up and away.

Moral: Everyone should be kind to all living things.
