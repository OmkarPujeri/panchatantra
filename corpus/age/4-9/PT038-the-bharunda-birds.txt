The Bharunda Birds

In a beautiful lake lived special birds called Bharunda Birds.
They had one tummy and two long necks each.

One day, a Bharunda Bird saw sweet nectar on the ground.
He wanted to eat it all himself.

His other neck said, "Please share it with me."
The first neck said, "No, I want it all."

The second neck got angry and found poisonous food.
He ate it and felt very sick.

Both necks belonged to the same bird, so they both died.

Moral: Sharing with others is kind and makes everyone happy.
