Spot's Captivity

There was a deer named Spot. 
He loved to run with his friends. 
One day, he got caught in a hunter's snare. 
The hunter took him home to the prince.

The prince gave Spot nice food and baths. 
He even gave him perfumes and ointments. 
But Spot missed his friends and home. 
He longed to run with them again.

Spot remembered his herd and said: 
"When shall I follow on the herd 
Of coursing deer again? 
When brace myself against the wind 
That whistles by? Ah, when?"

The prince got scared and thought Spot was a magic deer. 
He called the sorcerers and magicians to help him. 
But a kind holy man came and said: 
"Dear sir, Spot is just a deer who misses his friends."

The holy man told the prince to set Spot free. 
The prince did, and Spot was happy. 
He ran back to his friends, and they were all happy together.

Slow, the turtle, was caught by the hunter. 
Gold, the mouse, came up with a plan to save him. 
They told Spot to pretend to be dead, 
and Swift to pretend to peck out Spot's eyes.

The hunter threw the turtle on the ground and ran for a club. 
Spot ran away, Swift flew into a tree, 
and the turtle escaped into the water. 
Gold slipped into a grass-clump.

The hunter was confused and ran away. 
The four friends were happy and free. 
They lived happily ever after.

Moral: True friends help each other in times of need.
