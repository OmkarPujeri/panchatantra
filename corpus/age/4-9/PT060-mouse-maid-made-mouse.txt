Mouse-Maid Made Mouse

There was a hermitage by a river.

Many holy men lived there. They were very holy.

They did not eat much. They drank very little water.

Their clothes were made of tree bark.

Yajnavalkya was the leader of the hermitage.

He bathed in the river and washed his mouth.

A little mouse fell from a hawk's beak.

Yajnavalkya caught the mouse and cleaned it.

He made the mouse a girl with magic power.

Yajnavalkya took the girl to his home.

His wife was happy to have a daughter.

She said, "Let's take care of her."

The girl grew up. She was twelve years old.

The mother said, "It's time for her to marry."

Yajnavalkya said, "Let's find a good husband for her."

The mother said, "Let's find someone with good wealth."

Yajnavalkya said, "Let's find someone with good family."

The mother said, "Let's find someone with good knowledge."

Yajnavalkya said, "Let's find someone with good position."

The mother said, "Let's find someone with good truth."

Yajnavalkya said, "I will find someone with all these qualities."

He summoned the sun. "Be kind, sun. Marry our daughter."

The girl said, "No, father. He is too hot."

Yajnavalkya said, "Is there someone better than you, sun?"

The sun said, "Yes, the cloud is better."

Yajnavalkya summoned the cloud. "Be kind, cloud. Marry our daughter."

The girl said, "No, father. He is too dark."

Yajnavalkya said, "Is there someone better than you, cloud?"

The cloud said, "Yes, the wind is better."

Yajnavalkya summoned the wind. "Be kind, wind. Marry our daughter."

The girl said, "No, father. He is too fidgety."

Yajnavalkya said, "Is there someone better than you, wind?"

The wind said, "Yes, the mountain is better."

Yajnavalkya summoned the mountain. "Be kind, mountain. Marry our daughter."

The girl said, "No, father. He is too rough."

Yajnavalkya said, "Is there someone better than you, mountain?"

The mountain said, "Yes, mice are better."

Yajnavalkya summoned a mouse. "Be kind, mouse. Marry our daughter."

The girl said, "Yes, father. I like him."

Yajnavalkya turned the girl into a mouse.

He gave her to the mouse. They lived happily.

Moral: Be happy with what you have.
