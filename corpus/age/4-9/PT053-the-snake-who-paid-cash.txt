The Snake Who Paid Cash

There was a Brahman who worked in a field.

He was very hot and dozed under a tree.

He saw a big snake with a big hood.

The Brahman thought, "This snake is special. I never said hello."

He begged some milk and went to the snake.

He said, "Hello, snake! I'm sorry I didn't say hello before."

He left the milk and went home.

Next morning, he found a gold coin in the milk saucer.

He went every day, gave milk, and got a coin each time.

One day, the Brahman went to town and sent his son.

The son took milk and left it at the ant-hill.

The next day, the son found one coin and thought, "The snake has money! I'll hit it with a stick and get all the coins!"

The son hit the snake, but it didn't die.

The snake bit the son with its sharp fangs.

The son died, and the relatives cremated him.

The Brahman came back and heard the story.

He said, "We should be kind to all living things."

Moral: Be kind to all living things.
