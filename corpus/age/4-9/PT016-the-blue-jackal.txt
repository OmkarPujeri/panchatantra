The Blue Jackal

 
Fierce-Howl was a hungry jackal. He roamed the city at night.

He entered a house where a dyer worked. Fierce-Howl fell into a big vat of blue dye.

The dogs that chased him went home. Fierce-Howl crawled out of the vat and escaped into the forest.

The animals saw Fierce-Howl's blue body. They were scared and ran away. They said, "This creature is strange. We don't know what it can do."

Fierce-Howl said, "Don't be afraid. I am your king. I will protect you."

The animals bowed to Fierce-Howl. He made a lion his prime minister and a tiger his lord of the bedchamber.

Fierce-Howl made the other animals do jobs for him. He ate food that the other animals hunted.

One day, Fierce-Howl heard his fellow jackals howling. He was happy and started to howl too.

The other animals saw that Fierce-Howl was a jackal. They realized they had been tricked. They said, "Kill the jackal!"

A tiger caught Fierce-Howl and tore him apart. Fierce-Howl died.

Moral: Be careful of strangers who pretend to be friends.
