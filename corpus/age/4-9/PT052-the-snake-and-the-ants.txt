The Snake and the Ants

There was a big black snake named Haughty.
He lived in an ant-hill.
One day, he tried to crawl through a small hole.
It was too small for his big body.

He got hurt and had a big wound.
The ants smelled the blood and came to see.
They were very angry and started to sting Haughty.
He tried to kill them, but they were too many.

The ants stung him again and again.
Haughty was very sad and weak.
He couldn't move and couldn't escape.
The ants kept stinging him until he died.

Moral: Many small creatures can defeat a single big creature.
