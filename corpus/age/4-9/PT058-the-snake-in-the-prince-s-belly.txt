The Snake in the Prince's Belly

There was a king named Godlike.
He had a son who was very sick.
A snake lived in the prince's belly.
The prince was sad and went to another country.

He begged for food in a big temple.
A kind king named Gift lived there.
He had two daughters, Red-Face and Ugly-Mug.
Red-Face said, "Victory, O King."
Ugly-Mug said, "Your deserts, O King."

The king got angry with Ugly-Mug.
He gave her to the prince in the temple.
Ugly-Mug was happy and married the prince.
She went shopping with her maids.

When she came back, she saw the snake.
The snake's head came out of the prince's mouth.
Another snake came out of an ant-hill.
The snakes saw each other and got angry.

The ant-hill snake said, "You are bad!"
The belly-snake said, "You are bad too!"
The ant-hill snake said, "Drink black mustard!"
The belly-snake said, "Pour in hot water!"

Ugly-Mug heard the snakes and did what they said.
The prince got better and was happy.
Ugly-Mug got rich and was honored.
She lived happily with her family.

Moral: Be quick to help those in need.
