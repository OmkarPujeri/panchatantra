How the Birds Picked a King

The birds of the forest gathered together.

They were swans, cranes, nightingales, and many more.

They said, "We need a king. But Garuda is busy with his god. He doesn't help us when we need him."

The birds looked around and saw the owl.

"Let's make the owl our king," they said.

They prepared a throne and brought water from holy streams.

They gave the owl a special dress and made him sit on the throne.

Just then, a crow came in. He was very clever.

The birds said, "Let's ask the crow what he thinks."

The crow said, "No, no! You can't make the owl king. He's ugly and mean. He's not a good choice."

The birds asked the crow why not.

The crow said, "We already have a good king, Garuda. Why do we need another one?"

The birds thought about what the crow said.

"He is right," the birds said. "We do not need a second king."

So the birds flew home to think some more.

But the owl stayed. He sat on the throne and waited.

"Why is the crowning so late?" he called.

His wife said, "The crow stopped the crowning. All the birds have flown away."

The owl was very angry. He said to the crow, "You stopped my crowning. From today, we are enemies."

Then the owl went home with his wife.

And from that day, crows and owls have been enemies.

Moral: Harsh words can make an enemy for life.
