The Bird with Golden Dung

There was a big tree on a mountain.

A bird lived in the tree. Its poop was special.

One day, a hunter came to the tree. The bird dropped its poop, and it turned to gold!

The hunter was amazed. "Wow! I've been hunting birds for 80 years, and I never saw gold in a bird's poop!"

The hunter set a trap in the tree. The bird forgot to be careful and sat on the trap. He got caught!

The hunter freed the bird from the trap and put him in a cage. He took the bird home.

The hunter thought, "What to do with this bird? If people find out, the king might get angry. I'll take the bird to the king myself."

The king saw the bird and was very happy. "Take good care of this bird. Give him all his favorite food and water."

A wise counselor said, "Wait! We don't know if the bird's poop really has gold. Let's set him free."

The king listened to the counselor and set the bird free. The bird flew away and sat on the king's doorway.

It dropped its poop again, and it turned to gold! The bird said, "I was foolish to trust the hunter. Then the hunter was foolish to catch me. And the king and the counselor were foolish to doubt me."

The bird flew away, free and happy.

Moral: Being foolish can lead to trouble, but also to freedom.
