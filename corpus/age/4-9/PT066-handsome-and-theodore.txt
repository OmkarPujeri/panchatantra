Handsome and Theodore

Two friends lived in a well and a hole.

Theodore was a frog-king. He was very sad. His relatives were mean. He wanted to get rid of them.

He met a black snake named Handsome. Handsome lived in the hole. Theodore thought, "I'll invite Handsome into the well. He will eat my relatives."

Theodore called out to Handsome, "Come out! Come out!" Handsome didn't know who was calling him. He thought, "Maybe it's a conjurer or a druggist who wants to catch me."

Theodore said, "I'm a frog-king named Theodore. I want to be friends with you." Handsome was surprised. He said, "Why would a frog and a snake be friends?"

Theodore said, "I was insulted by my relatives. I want to make friends with you to get rid of them." Handsome agreed to help.

Theodore showed Handsome an easy way into the well. Handsome lay down in the hole and ate all of Theodore's relatives.

Theodore said, "Now you're hungry. Let me give you something to eat." Handsome said, "No, you will give me a frog every day. If not, I will eat all of you."

Theodore was worried. He thought, "If I give him frogs every day, he will eat all of us." He decided to give Handsome a frog a day.

But Handsome ate more frogs than he should have. He ate a frog named Theodosius, who was Theodore's son.

Theodore was very sad. His wife said, "Think of a plan to escape or kill him." Theodore planned his escape and left the well.

Handsone said, "My dear Theodore, I'm hungry. Please give me something to eat." Theodore said, "I'll go find some frogs from other wells and bring them here."

Handsone said, "Well, I can't eat you. You are like a brother. But if you bring more frogs, I will eat them." Theodore left the well.

A lizard said to Handsone, "Theodore is an old friend of mine. I will find him and tell him to come back." The lizard found Theodore and said, "Your friend Handsone is waiting for you. Please come back."

Theodore said, "Tell Handsone that I will never come back to the well. I'm not afraid of him." The lizard went back to Handsone and told him.

Handsone said, "My dear Theodore, please come back. I will never hurt you." Theodore said, "No, I will never come back."

Moral: Don't trust people who are too powerful.
