The Duel between Elephant and Sparrow

In a jungle, a sparrow and his wife lived in a nest.

A big elephant came to the jungle with a bad fever. He was very hot and thirsty. He saw the sparrow's nest and pulled it with his trunk, breaking it. The sparrow's eggs were hurt, but the parents were safe.

The sparrow's wife was very sad. She cried and cried. A woodpecker friend came and said, "Don't cry. What's the use?"

The sparrow's wife said, "But my babies were killed by that big elephant. I'm so sad." The woodpecker said, "I have a plan. Let's get a gnat friend to help us." The woodpecker and the sparrow's wife went to find the gnat.

The gnat said, "I have a frog friend who can help us. Let's call him." The three friends went to find the frog. The frog said, "I have a plan. The elephant is sick and thirsty. If we make him think he finds water, he will fall into a pit and die."

The friends carried out the plan. The gnat sang a happy song in the elephant's ear. The woodpecker pecked out the elephant's eyes. The frog croaked loudly, making the elephant thirsty. The elephant followed the sound and fell into a deep pit.

The elephant died. The sparrow's wife was happy that her babies' killer was gone.

Moral: Even the strongest can be defeated with clever planning.
