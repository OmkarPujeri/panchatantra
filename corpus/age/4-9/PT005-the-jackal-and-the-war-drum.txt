The Jackal and the War-Drum

There was a hungry jackal one day.

He wandered into a forest searching for food.

He came to a king's battle ground and heard a big sound.

The jackal felt sad and worried. "What's happening?" he thought.

He looked around and saw a big war-drum.

The drum was making a loud noise. "Who made this sound?" the jackal wondered.

He saw that the drum was quiet when the wind was still.

When the wind blew, the grass swayed and struck the drum.

That was what made the sound.

The jackal crept closer and saw that the drum was made of wood and leather.

He thought, "This drum must be full of meat and fat!"

The jackal was excited and decided to take a closer look.

He gnawed a hole in the drum and crawled inside.

But when he looked around, he was very disappointed.

He saw only wood and skin inside the drum.

The jackal laughed and said, "I was so silly! I thought it was full of food!"

Moral: Don't judge something by its noise or appearance.
