The Cave That Talked

There was a hungry lion named Rough-Claw. He had no food to eat. He was very thirsty.

Rough-Claw found a big cave in a mountain. He went inside to rest. The cave belonged to a jackal named Curd-Face. Curd-Face came to the cave door and sang: "Hello! Cave ahoy!"

Rough-Claw thought: "This cave always calls out a greeting. But today, it's silent. I will call out a greeting." Rough-Claw roared loudly.

The cave's echo was very loud. It scared other animals far away. Curd-Face ran away, laughing.

Curd-Face met a friend named Red-Eye. Red-Eye said: "I know why you ran away. You were scared of the lion." Curd-Face said: "Yes, I was scared."

Red-Eye met another friend named Live-Strong. Live-Strong said: "I know why you ran away. You were scared of the lion." Red-Eye said: "No, I was scared because I knew the lion was hungry."

Now that Red-Eye was gone, Live-Strong was glad. He thought: "Red-Eye was the clever one. Now I can destroy the owls."

Each day, Live-Strong carried one stick of wood to the gate. He piled the wood up high. The owls did not see his plan.

One morning, the owls could not see at all. Owls go blind in the day. Cloudy, the crow-king, came with his friends. Each bird carried a burning stick. They threw the fire on the woodpile. The cave burned, and all the owls died. Cloudy had beaten his enemies.

Moral: Don't trust your enemies, and be careful who you trust.
