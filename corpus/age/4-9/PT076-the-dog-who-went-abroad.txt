The Dog Who Went Abroad

There was a dog named Spot in a town with a big famine. Food was scarce. Dogs and others lost their homes.

Spot felt very hungry. He thought, "I must go somewhere else." So he went to another country.

In a new city, Spot found a messy house. The woman who lived there was not good at keeping her home clean. Spot went in every day and ate a lot of good food.

But when Spot left, other big dogs came. They were mean and hurt Spot badly.

Spot thought, "Going abroad is not good. I want to go back home." So he went back to his own town.

His family asked, "Tell us about your trip. What was the food like? How did the people behave?"

Spot said, "The food was very good. There were many kinds to eat. The women were nice and friendly. But the men in that town were not nice. They were mean."

Spot's family was happy to see him. They were glad he was back home.

Moral: It's always better to be at home.
