Mother Shandilee's Bargain

A Brahman and his wife lived in a town.

The Brahman was a kind man. He gave shelter to guests.

One rainy day, I asked for shelter. He gave it to me.

I lived with them, doing good deeds.

One day, I woke up early. I heard the Brahman and his wife talking.

The Brahman said, "Tomorrow is the winter solstice. I will go to another village to collect donations."

His wife said, "Who will give food to a poor Brahman like you? You should be ashamed."

The Brahman was scared. He said, "My dear, don't say that. Even if you have little, give something to those in need."

The Brahman told a story. "A cloud gives only water, but the world treats it with respect. The sun gives much, but no one likes it."

The Brahman's wife said, "I have some sesame at home. I will make flour from it and feed a Brahman." The Brahman went to another village. The wife took the shells off the sesame. She dried it in the sun. But a dog made water on the grain. "This grain is spoiled," she thought. "But it still looks clean. I will trade it with a neighbor." She went from house to house. A neighbor was glad to trade. Later the neighbor's husband came home. He looked at the grain. He said, "Mother Shandilee is very clever at a bargain. Throw this sesame away!"

Moral: A clever bargain can trick the person who trades with you.
