Loss of Gains

Red-Face the monkey lived in a rose-apple tree.
The tree grew beside the sea.
Its fruit was sweet like honey.
One day a crocodile named Ugly-Mug came.
He crawled onto the sand under the tree.
"Welcome, friend," said Red-Face.
"Please eat these fruits. They taste like nectar."
Ugly-Mug ate the fruit. It was very sweet.
Every day the two friends sat and talked.
Red-Face gave him more fruit to take home.

The crocodile's wife ate the fruit too.
"Where do you get these sweet fruits?" she asked.
"My friend the monkey gives them to me," he said.
"Then his heart must be full of nectar," she said.
"Bring me his heart."
"But he is my friend," said the crocodile.
"I do not want to hurt him."
His wife grew angry. "You must do it!" she said.
She would not eat until he agreed.
So the sad crocodile went back to the tree.
"My wife wants to meet you," he told the monkey.
"Come home with me. Climb on my back."
Red-Face trusted his friend. He climbed on.

The crocodile swam far out into the deep sea.
Red-Face was afraid of the big waves.
"My friend," said the crocodile, "I must tell you the truth.
I am taking you to my wife.
She has asked me to bring her your heart."
Red-Face was very scared. But he was clever too.
"Oh no!" he said. "Why did you not tell me before?
I left my heart at home, in a hole in the tree.
Take me back and I will bring it."
The crocodile believed him.
He swam back to the shore.
Red-Face jumped off and climbed his tree.
He was safe high in the branches.
"You silly crocodile!" he laughed.
"Who ever has two hearts?
Go away. Never come back."
The crocodile felt very foolish.
He swam away and never came back again.

Later, another crocodile took over his home.
The sad crocodile asked Red-Face what to do.
"Be brave and fight for your home," said the monkey.
So the crocodile fought the stranger and won.
He got his home back and lived there happily.

Moral: A quick mind can save you when you are in danger.
