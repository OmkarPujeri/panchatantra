The Lion and the Ram

A ram was lost from his flock. He roamed the forest alone.

The lion saw the ram. He was afraid of the ram's big horns and thick fleece.

The lion thought, "This ram is very strong. He can eat and run freely."

Another day, the lion saw the ram eating grass on the ground.

The lion thought, "If he eats grass, he must not be strong. He is weak."

The lion was wrong. He attacked the ram and hurt him.

The ram was too weak to defend himself. He was killed by the lion.

Moral: Don't judge others by what you see on the surface.
