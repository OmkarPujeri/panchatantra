The Jackal at the Ram-Fight

There was a wise man named Godly.

He had a kind and clever disciple named June.

Godly was very happy with June.

He saw two rams fighting in a field.

Their heads crashed together, and blood came out.

A hungry jackal saw the blood and came closer.

He stood between the two rams and drank the blood.

Godly saw the jackal and thought, "This jackal is very foolish."

The next time the rams fought, the jackal stayed.

The rams crashed together, and the jackal died.

Godly said, "The jackal at the ram-fight" and felt sad.

He looked for his treasure but it was gone.

He searched everywhere but couldn't find it.

He fell down and cried, "Oh no! I'm robbed!"

He got up and shouted, "June, where did you go?"

He followed June's tracks, muttering to himself.

He said, "We were tricked by June, just like the jackal."

Moral: Greed and foolishness lead to trouble.
