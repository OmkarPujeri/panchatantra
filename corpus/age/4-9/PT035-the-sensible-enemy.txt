The Sensible Enemy

Two friends, a merchant's son and a man of learning's son, met a prince.

The prince did not like to ride horses or shoot arrows. He showed his friends how sad he was.

His friends said, "We also don't like our fathers' work. We're sad too."

The prince said, "Let's leave and find a new place to live." He wanted to travel.

The merchant's son said, "Let's go to Climbing Mountain. We can find precious gems there."

The prince and his friends went to Climbing Mountain. Each of them found a beautiful gem.

They said, "How will we keep our gems safe?" The man of learning's son said, "We'll swallow them. Then they won't be stolen."

They ate their gems for dinner. But a man had been watching them. He wanted to steal their gems.

He said, "I'll travel with them and cut their stomachs open when they sleep."

The prince and his friends welcomed the man to join them. They continued their journey.

As they walked, they passed by a village. A bird in a cage sang a song. The village chief heard the song.

He said, "The bird says there are gems in the travelers' stomachs. Catch them!"

The chief's men caught the prince and his friends. But they didn't find any gems.

The chief said, "I'll inspect them again." The bird sang the same song. The chief caught them again.

The prince and his friends said, "If there are gems in our stomachs, why didn't you find them?"

The chief said, "The bird never lies. I'll cut your stomachs open in the morning." He put them in a dungeon.

The thief said, "I'll save my friends by offering my stomach first." He asked the chief to cut his stomach open first.

The chief agreed. But he found no gem in the thief's stomach. He was ashamed.

He said, "I've done a terrible thing. I'll set them free." The prince and his friends were happy.

Moral: A sensible enemy can be kind and save the day.
