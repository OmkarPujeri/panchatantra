The Lion-Makers

Four friends, Brahman friends, lived together.

They were very learned, but not very wise.

One said, "We must go travel, meet kings, and get money."

But the eldest said, "Our friend has only sense, not learning."

So he said, "Let's not share our money with him. He must go home."

The second one said, "You have no learning. Go home."

But the third one said, "We're friends. He can come with us."

They went on a journey, and found a lion's bones.

"Let's bring this lion back to life," said one of them.

"We can make it new," said another.

But the wise one said, "No, it's a lion. It will kill us."

The others said, "Don't worry, we'll be safe."

They brought the lion to life, and it rose up.

It killed the three friends, and went away.

The wise one climbed down and went home.

Moral: Sense is more important than learning.
