The Ungrateful Wife

There was a kind Brahman who loved his wife.

He left his family to live in a faraway place.

His wife said, "I'm thirsty, please find water."

He searched and found water, but she was already dead.

He was sad, but a voice from heaven said, "Give half your life, and she'll live."

He gave half his life, and his wife stood up.

They drank water and ate fruits, and then they walked.

The Brahman left his wife in a flower garden.

A cripple was turning a water wheel and singing.

His wife fell in love with the cripple and married him.

She asked the Brahman to take the cripple with them.

The Brahman agreed, thinking it was a good idea.

But the wife and the cripple pushed the Brahman into a well.

The wife took the cripple and went to the city.

The king saw the cripple in a basket and asked what it meant.

The wife said the cripple was her invalid husband.

The king gave her two villages and said she could enjoy them with her husband.

The Brahman arrived in the city and was accused by his wife.

The king sentenced him to death.

The Brahman said, "She has something that belongs to me. Make her give it back."

The wife said she had nothing, but the Brahman knew she had half his life.

She said the magic words, "I give life," and fell dead.

Moral: Greed and selfishness can lead to great trouble.
