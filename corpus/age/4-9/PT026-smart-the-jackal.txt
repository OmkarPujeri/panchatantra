Smart, the Jackal

In a forest lived a lion named Thunder-Fang.
He had three friends: Meat-Face the wolf, Smart the jackal, and Spike-Ear the camel.

Thunder-Fang fought with a big elephant and got hurt.
He was very hungry and weak.

"Find some food for me," he said to his friends.
But they couldn't find any.

Smart thought, "I can trick them into eating Spike-Ear."
He said to Spike-Ear, "Master is very hungry. If you give your body, he will live."

Spike-Ear said, "I will do it. It's a good deed."

Thunder-Fang agreed. Spike-Ear gave his body to be eaten.

Smart said, "Master is tired. He should go bathe in the river."
When Thunder-Fang went, Smart said to Meat-Face, "Eat the camel, but don't tell Master."

Meat-Face ate a little, but Smart said, "Stop! Master is coming."

Thunder-Fang came back and saw the camel was hurt.
He was very angry.

Meat-Face ran away. Thunder-Fang chased him, but then stopped.

A big camel caravan came. Thunder-Fang heard the bells.

Smart said, "Run, Master! The Death-God is coming to punish you."

Thunder-Fang ran away. Smart ate the camel.

Moral: Always be kind and honest, not selfish and mean.
