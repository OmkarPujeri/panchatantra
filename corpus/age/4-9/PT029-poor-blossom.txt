Poor Blossom

There was a parrot named Blossom. He lived with God Indra.

Blossom was very smart and beautiful. He loved to talk.

One day, God Indra was resting. Blossom was on his hand. He was happy and talking.

Then he saw Yama, the god of death. Blossom moved away. The other gods asked, "Why did you move away?"

Blossom said, "Yama brings harm to all living things. Why not move away from him?" The gods were worried for Blossom.

They asked Yama not to kill Blossom. Yama said, "It's not my decision. Time decides these things."

The gods took Blossom to meet Time. Time said, "Death is in charge. Ask him."

They asked Death, but Blossom died just by seeing him. Everyone was sad.

The gods asked Yama, "Why did Blossom die?" Yama said, "It was his fate to die when he saw Death."

Moral: Sometimes things happen because it's meant to be.
