Right-Mind and Wrong-Mind

Two friends, Right-Mind and Wrong-Mind, lived in a city.

They were sons of merchants and wanted to earn money.

They traveled to another country to make money.

Right-Mind found a pot with 1000 dinars.

He told Wrong-Mind, "We can go home now."

Wrong-Mind said, "Let's take 100 dinars each and bury the rest."

Right-Mind agreed. Each took 100 dinars.

They hid the rest in the ground and went home.

Wrong-Mind spent his 100 dinars quickly.

He asked Right-Mind to divide the rest.

Right-Mind said, "Let's take 200 dinars each."

Wrong-Mind took the rest and hid it.

A month later, Wrong-Mind went to Right-Mind.

He said, "Let's divide the rest equally."

They dug, but found nothing.

Wrong-Mind accused Right-Mind of stealing.

Right-Mind said, "I didn't steal it. I'm Right-Mind."

They took their dispute to court.

The magistrates decided to test them.

Wrong-Mind said, "I have a witness, the goddess of the wood."

The magistrates said, "Bring the goddess tomorrow."

Wrong-Mind went home and told his father.

He asked his father to be his witness.

His father said, "This is a bad plan. Think carefully."

The next day, they all went to the tree.

The magistrates asked, "Goddess, who took the money?"

A voice from the tree said, "Right-Mind took it."

Right-Mind set fire to the tree.

Wrong-Mind's father ran out of the hole.

His body was scorched.

He said, "It is all Wrong-Mind's doing."

The trick was exposed.

Wrong-Mind had to give back the stolen money.

Right-Mind was free.

Moral: Think carefully before doing something.
