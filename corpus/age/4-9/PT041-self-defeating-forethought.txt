Self-defeating Forethought

Two hunters were once out in the forest.

Red-Face was hunting a big boar. He saw the boar and drew his bow.

He said, "I will shoot this boar and eat its meat."

Red-Face shot the boar with an arrow. But the boar was very strong.

The boar fought back and hurt Red-Face badly. Both the hunter and the boar died.

A hungry jackal came to the spot. He saw the dead hunter and the dead boar.

The jackal thought, "This is a good luck for me. I will eat and be happy."

The jackal started eating the sinew of the bow. He thought, "I will eat slowly and have food for many days."

But as he ate, the bowstring snapped. The bow-tip went into his mouth and hurt him badly.

The jackal died from the pain. Both the hunter and the jackal died because of their own mistakes.

Moral: Greed can be very bad for you.
