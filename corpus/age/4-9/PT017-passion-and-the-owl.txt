Passion and the Owl

There was a big lake in a forest. A king-swan named Passion lived there.

Passion was a happy swan. He loved to play and have fun. One day, an owl came to visit him.

The owl said, "I heard you are a kind swan. I want to be friends with you." Passion said, "You're welcome to stay with me by the lake."

The owl and Passion played together every day. The owl said, "I have to go home now. Please visit me at Lotus Grove." Passion said, "I'll come and see you soon."

But Passion never went to visit the owl. He thought the owl's home would be boring. He wanted to play and have fun with the owl by the lake.

One day, Passion went to visit the owl. But the owl was hiding in a dark hole. The owl said, "I'm blind during the day. Meet me at night."

Passion waited for the owl at night. They said hello, and Passion went to sleep. The next morning, a caravan came to the lake. The owl hooted loudly, but Passion didn't wake up.

The caravan leader thought the owl's hoot was a bad sign. He ordered his archer to shoot the swan. The archer shot and killed Passion.

Moral: Harsh words and sudden actions can cause harm.
