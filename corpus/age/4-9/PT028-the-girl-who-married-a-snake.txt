The Girl Who Married a Snake

In a faraway city, a Brahman named Godly lived. He and his wife had no children.

One day, Godly said, "Don't worry, my dear. I had a special dream. We will have a very special son."

The wife was happy. She waited and waited. Then she had a baby. But when she saw him, she was shocked. He was a snake!

The neighbors said, "Throw him away!" But the wife loved him. She took care of him, fed him milk and butter.

As the snake grew, the wife loved him more and more. She said, "I want a marriage festival for my boy!"

Godly said, "Who would give their daughter to a snake?" But he loved his wife, so he went on a long journey to find a wife for the snake.

He traveled to a faraway city called Kutkuta City. He met a kind host who gave him food and shelter.

The next morning, Godly said, "I'm looking for a wife for my son." His host said, "I have a beautiful daughter. Take her!"

Godly took the girl to his home. But when people saw her, they said, "A snake? She's too beautiful for him!"

The girl's relatives said, "Take her away from the snake!" But the girl said, "No! I will marry him!"

That night, the snake came out of his box. He was not a snake now. He was a handsome man. The girl was scared at first. "Do not be afraid," he said. "I am your husband." Then he went back into the snake skin. He came out as a man again. Now she knew it was true. She was very happy. They hugged each other. In the morning, Godly saw the snake skin. He threw it into the fire. "Now my son can never be a snake again," he said. Godly and his wife showed everyone their handsome son.

Moral: Be brave and happy with what you have.
