Soft, the Weaver

In a town, a weaver named Soft lived. He made beautiful clothes. But he never had enough money.

His wife said, "Don't move. You'll be okay here."

Soft said, "No, I'll go to Growing City. I'll earn more money."

He went to Growing City and stayed three years. He earned 300 gold-pieces and started home.

In a forest, he climbed a banyan tree to sleep. He saw two men arguing.

One said, "You gave Soft 300 gold-pieces. Why did you do that?"

The other said, "I give rewards to those who work hard. It's not my problem."

Soft woke up and found his bag empty. He thought, "Oh no! I worked hard for nothing."

He went back to Growing City and earned 500 gold-pieces in one year. He started home again.

In the forest, he saw the same two men arguing again.

One said, "You gave Soft 500 gold-pieces. He doesn't get anything beyond food and clothes."

The other said, "I give rewards to those who work hard. It's not my problem."

Soft woke up again and found his bag empty. He thought, "I'll just hang myself from this tree."

But a figure appeared in the sky and said, "Don't be so rash, Soft. I'm the one who takes your money. You can go home. Ask for what you want."

Soft said, "Give me plenty of money."

The figure said, "What will you do with money if you can't enjoy it or give it away? You can only use it for food and clothes."

Soft said, "I still want it. It will make me look important."

The figure said, "Go to Growing City. Two rich men live there, Penny-Hide and Penny-Fling. Watch them. Then choose which man you want to be."

The figure vanished. Soft went back to Growing City.

He went to Penny-Hide's house. They gave him food, but no kind words. That night he saw the two men again. In the morning he had no food, because Penny-Hide was sick.

So Soft went to Penny-Fling's house. Penny-Fling welcomed him. He gave Soft food, clothes, and a soft bed. At dawn, a man came from the king with money. He gave it all to Penny-Fling.

Soft thought, "Penny-Fling gives and enjoys. He is better than stingy Penny-Hide." So Soft asked to be a man whose money brings gifts and joy. And the Lord of All made him that kind of man.

Moral: Money is a gift to share, not a treasure to hide.
