The Loss of Friends

There was a merchant named Increase.
He lived in a city called Maidens' Delight.
It was a beautiful city with many temples and a big moat.

Increase was a kind merchant with a lot of money.
He wanted to earn more money, so he went on a journey.
He had two strong bulls, Joyful and Lively, to help him carry his goods.

As they traveled, Lively got tired and fell into the mud.
The driver said, "Lively is sick, my lord."
Increase was sad and stayed in the forest for five nights.

But Lively was not dead. He got up and ran to the river.
He drank water and ate grass, and soon he was healthy again.

Increase left the forest, but the men with him lied to him.
They said, "Lively died, and we performed the last rites."
Increase was sad, but he continued his journey.

Lively met a lion named Rusty, who was the king of the forest.
Rusty was proud and strong, and he ruled his kingdom with kindness.

Two jackals, Cheek and Victor, were friends of Rusty.
They had no work now, and they were jealous of Lively.

Victor was the mean one. He made a sly plan.
He told Rusty a lie. He said, "Lively wants to hurt you."
Then he told Lively a lie. He said, "Rusty wants to hurt you."
The two friends grew angry and afraid of each other.
They fought in the forest.
Rusty struck Lively. Lively died.
Rusty was very sad. He cried.
He said, "Lively was my best friend. I have only hurt myself."

Moral: Lying and being jealous can lead to loss of friends.
