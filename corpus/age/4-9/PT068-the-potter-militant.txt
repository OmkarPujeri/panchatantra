The Potter Militant

There was a potter. He was very careless.

One day, he ran into a broken pot and fell. He got a big cut on his forehead.

The cut became a big, ugly scar.

The land had a big famine. The potter was very hungry. He joined the king's guards.

The king saw the potter's scar and thought he was a great hero. He gave the potter many gifts and honors.

The king's princes were very jealous. They didn't say anything, but they were angry.

The king had a big review of his army. He asked the potter, "What's your name? What's your family?"

The potter said, "My name is Fight-Firm. I'm a potter. I fell and hurt my forehead when I was drunk."

The king was very surprised. He thought, "I was tricked by this potter!"

The king was angry and said, "You must leave the army."

The potter said, "Please don't be angry. Let me show you my bravery!"

The king said, "No, you must go. Your family is not brave like you think."

The king told the potter a story. Then he said, "You must go too, before they find out you are a potter. If not, they will hoot you and kill you." The potter heard this and ran away.

Moral: Honesty is always the best policy.
