Hang-Ball and Greedy

A long time ago, in a small town, there lived a big, strong bull named Hang-Ball.

Hang-Ball loved to run and play in the forest, eating delicious grasses and drinking from the river.

In the same forest, a sly jackal named Greedy lived with his wife. One day, they were lying on a sandy riverbank, enjoying the sunshine.

Hang-Ball came to the river to drink, and the she-jackal saw his long, hanging testicles. She said to her husband, "Look! Those big balls will fall soon. Let's follow him."

But Greedy was not interested. "Why bother?" he said. "Maybe they will fall, maybe not. We have plenty of mice to eat here. If I follow Hang-Ball, someone else will come and take our spot."

His wife scolded him, "You are lazy and afraid. We should be brave and strong. If we follow Hang-Ball, we might get some good food."

Greedy listened to his wife and followed Hang-Ball. They spent many days walking behind the bull, but those hanging testicles never fell.

After 15 long years, Greedy was tired and said to his wife, "I've watched them for 15 years, and they're still hanging. I think they will never fall. Let's go back to our old home and eat mice again."

Moral: Sometimes, we waste our time and energy following something that might not happen.
