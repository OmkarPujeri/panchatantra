Flop-Ear and Dusty

Dusty, a faithful jackal, served the mighty lion, Fierce-Mane, who lived in a forest. One day, the lion was badly injured in a fight with an elephant and couldn't move.

Dusty, starving and weak, begged the lion to find him a prey to kill. The lion told him to hunt an animal that he could kill even in his injured state. Dusty went to a nearby village and found a donkey named Flop-Ear, who was struggling to survive on thin grass.

Flop-Ear told Dusty that he was mistreated by the laundryman and longed for a better life. Dusty promised him a lovely spot by a river with green grass and three beautiful she-donkeys who would be his companions. Flop-Ear was convinced and followed Dusty to the lion's presence.

The lion, overjoyed to see his prey, sprang into action but landed on the other side, missing Flop-Ear. The donkey was terrified and ran away. Dusty returned to the lion and promised to bring another animal, but he secretly followed Flop-Ear and convinced him to return to the lion's presence.

This time, the lion was prepared and killed Flop-Ear. The lion went to the river to bathe and left Dusty on guard. Dusty ate the donkey's ears and heart. When the lion came back, he found the donkey minus ears and heart, and grew very angry. But Dusty said the donkey was born without ears and heart. The lion believed him, divided the food, and ate his share without suspicion.

Moral: Those who let sweet promises deceive them may lose their lives.
