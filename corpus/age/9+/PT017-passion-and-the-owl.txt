Passion and the Owl

In a forest near a vast lake, a king-swan named Passion lived a life of leisure. He spent his days engaging in various activities, but his life was about to change.

One day, a wise owl arrived at the lake, seeking the swan's friendship. The owl was impressed by Passion's reputation and wanted to learn from him. Passion welcomed the owl, and they spent their time together, enjoying each other's company.

However, as time passed, the owl asked Passion to visit his home, Lotus Grove. Passion, eager for new experiences, decided to pay his friend a visit. He traveled to Lotus Grove, but couldn't find the owl. When he finally discovered him, he was shocked to see the owl living in a small, dark hole.

The owl explained that he was blind during the day and only came out at night. Passion waited patiently for the owl to emerge, but when the sun set, he fell asleep, exhausted from his journey.

The next morning, a large caravan arrived at the lake. The leader gave the signal to depart by blowing a conch. The owl answered with a loud, harsh hoot, then dived into a hole. But the swan, still sleeping, did not stir. To the leader, the owl's harsh cry was a bad omen. Fear seized him, and he ordered an archer who could aim by sound to shoot. The archer drew his bow and killed the sleeping swan.

Moral: Harsh words and impulsive actions can lead to devastating consequences.
