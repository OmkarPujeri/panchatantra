The Jackal at the Ram-Fight

Godly, a wise teacher, rested peacefully, his mind at ease.

He had been observing his clever disciple, June, for a long time.

As he sat, he watched a herd of rams fighting fiercely. Two rams clashed, their hard foreheads crashing together, and blood flowed everywhere. A hungry jackal, driven by his love for meat, crept between the two rams, lapping up the blood.

Godly thought to himself, "This jackal is very foolish. If he stays between the rams when they crash, he'll surely get hurt or even killed."

The next time the rams fought, the jackal didn't move away. He stayed between the rams, and when they crashed, he was caught in the middle and died.

Godly was sad and said, "The jackal at the ram-fight." He then returned to his treasure, which was hidden nearby.

But when he arrived, he discovered that his treasure was gone. He panicked, fell to the ground, and cried out, "I've been robbed!" He quickly got up, looked around, and started to shout, "June, June! Where did you go after cheating me? Tell me the truth!"

Godly followed June's tracks, muttering to himself, "We all get tricked sometimes, just like June tricked me."

Moral: Greed and foolishness can lead to great harm, and we must learn from our mistakes.
