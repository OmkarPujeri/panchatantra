The Bird with Golden Dung

A great tree stood on a mountain side, home to a bird with a special gift.

Its dung contained gold, and one day a hunter stumbled upon this treasure. The bird dropped its dung right in front of the hunter, and it turned to gold at once. The hunter was amazed.

He thought, "I've been hunting birds for years, and never saw gold in their dung!" So, he set a snare in the tree, and the bird, foolishly, perched on the spot. The hunter caught the bird, but then freed it from the snare and put it in a cage.

The hunter took the bird home and told the king about its golden dung. The king was delighted and ordered his guards to care for the bird. They gave it the best food and drink, but a wise counselor spoke up.

"Wait a minute," he said. "This bird's story is hard to believe. We have no proof that its dung really contains gold. Let's set it free." The king listened and decided to release the bird.

The bird flew out of the cage and perched on the doorway, dropping its dung, which turned to gold once more. The bird looked at the king, the hunter, and the counselor, and said, "We all made fools of ourselves. You caught me, the king kept me, and the counselor freed me. We were all wrong!"

Moral: Even the wisest people can make mistakes, and it's always best to be cautious and think things through.
