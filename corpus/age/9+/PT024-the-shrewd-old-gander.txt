The Shrewd Old Gander

In a forest, a fig tree with massive branches stood tall. A flock of wild geese made it their home. At the root of the tree, a creeping vine called koshambi began to grow. The old gander warned the geese, "This vine is a threat. It could be used to climb up and harm us. Cut it down before it's too late."

But the geese laughed at the gander's words and ignored his advice. The vine grew stronger and wrapped itself around the tree. One day, while the geese were out foraging, a sneaky hunter climbed the tree using the vine. He laid a snare among the nests and waited for the geese to return.

When the geese came back at nightfall, they were caught in the snare. The old gander said, "I told you so. You ignored my warning, and now you're all trapped." The geese asked for his advice on what to do next. The old gander suggested, "When the hunter comes, pretend to be dead. When he throws the last one to the ground, we'll all rise together and fly away."

The hunter arrived at dawn, and seeing the geese lying still, he thought they were dead. He carefully freed them from the snare and threw them to the ground one by one. But the geese waited until the hunter was about to leave, then they followed the old gander's plan and flew up together, soaring over the hunter's head.

Moral: Heed the advice of older, wiser friends, for they often know what's best.
