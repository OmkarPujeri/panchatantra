Hang-Ball and Greedy

In a small town lived a wild bull named Hang-Ball. He was very strong and loved to roam freely in the forest.

A jackal named Greedy lived in the same forest with his wife. One day, they were lying on a sandy riverbank, enjoying the sun. Hang-Ball came down to the river for a drink, and the she-jackal spotted his long, hanging testicles.

"Look, my dear!" she exclaimed. "Those two lumps of flesh will fall soon, or maybe within a few hours. Let's follow him and see what happens!"

Greedy hesitated, "Maybe they will fall, maybe not. Why waste our time? I'd rather stay here with you and hunt the mice that come to drink. If I follow him, someone else might take our spot."

The she-jackal scolded him, "You're a coward, satisfied with small things. We should always be energetic and take risks. Remember, 'Depend on energetic might and banish indolence's blight.' Being lazy will only lead to trouble."

Greedy was convinced by his wife's words and left the riverbank to follow Hang-Ball. They spent many days tracking the bull, but the testicles never fell.

After 15 years, Greedy was tired and frustrated. "We've been following him for so long, but those testicles are still hanging. I think we should give up and go back to our old mouse-hunting routine."

The she-jackal agreed, and they returned to their usual spot. Greedy realized that his wife's constant goading had led him astray, and he should have been more careful.

Moral: A man should be careful not to let others' opinions or constant nagging lead him away from what's sensible and practical.
