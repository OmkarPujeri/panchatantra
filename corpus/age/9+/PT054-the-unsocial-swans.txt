The Unsocial Swans

There was once a king named Gay-Chariot who ruled over a kingdom. He owned a beautiful lake called Lotus Lake, which was guarded by his soldiers.

The lake was home to many golden swans, who gave one of their tail feathers to the king every six months. It was a tradition that had been going on for a long time.

One day, a magnificent golden bird arrived at the lake. The swans told him that he couldn't live among them because they had rented the lake for a price – one tail feather every six months.

The golden bird complained to the king, saying that the swans were being unfair and rude. He asked the king to intervene in the dispute.

The king, Gay-Chariot, listened to both sides and then gave an order to his soldiers: "Kill all the birds and bring them to me." The soldiers were about to carry out the king's command when one wise old bird had an idea.

He saw the soldiers approaching with clubs and said to the other birds, "Let's fly away together! We can't let them harm us." The birds quickly took to the skies and escaped.

Moral: Be generous to all that lives.
