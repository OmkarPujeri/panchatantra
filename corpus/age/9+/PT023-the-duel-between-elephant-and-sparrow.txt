The Duel between Elephant and Sparrow

In a dense jungle, a sparrow and his wife built their nest on a tamal tree branch. One day, a jungle elephant with spring fever sought shade beneath the tree. Blinded by his fever, he broke the branch with his trunk, crushing the sparrows' eggs. The parent birds barely escaped death.

The hen-sparrow was heartbroken over the loss of her chicks. A woodpecker friend visited her, trying to comfort her. "Don't grieve in vain," the woodpecker said. "Wise people don't lament what's lost. Fools cause more trouble by adding sadness to those who are already sad."

The hen-sparrow argued, "But this elephant killed my babies. If you're my friend, think of a plan to kill him. If that's done, I'll feel less grief." The woodpecker agreed and came up with a plan, involving a gnat, a frog, and a clever scheme to kill the elephant.

The woodpecker and the hen-sparrow went to find the gnat, Lute-Buzz. The gnat suggested involving Cloud-Messenger, a wise frog. Together, the three friends explained the situation to Cloud-Messenger. The frog proposed a plan to use the gnat's music, the woodpecker's bill, and the frog's croaking to lure the elephant to his death.

The plan was carried out, and the elephant, blinded by the woodpecker, fell into a pit and perished.

Moral: Even the strongest can be brought down by many small allies working one clever plan.
