Merchant Strong-Tooth

In the city of Growing City, a wise merchant named Strong-Tooth managed the administration. He was known for his cleverness and fairness. People loved him, and the king trusted him completely.

Strong-Tooth once had a daughter get married, and he invited the whole town and the king's entourage. He treated them with great honor, fed them, and gave them gifts. After the wedding, he escorted the king home, showing him great respect.

Bull, the king's lowly servant, was jealous of Strong-Tooth's position. He thought, "Why should I be angry? I won't hurt him. I'll just wait for the right moment." One morning, Bull saw Strong-Tooth near the king's bed and accused him of kissing the queen. The king believed Bull and withdrew his favor from Strong-Tooth.

Strong-Tooth was puzzled. He thought, "I've never done anything wrong. Why is the king angry with me?" He realized that Bull was behind the king's decision. Strong-Tooth reflected, "A lowly servant at the palace can still have honor and respect."

Strong-Tooth decided to clear his name. He summoned Bull and gave him two garments as a gift, explaining that he had driven him out because of his misbehavior. Bull was overjoyed and said, "Friend, I forgive you."

The next day, Bull entered the palace and made another false accusation against the king. The king realized his mistake and reinstated Strong-Tooth, presenting him with gems and garments.

Moral: A little thing can lift someone high, and a little thing can bring them down.
