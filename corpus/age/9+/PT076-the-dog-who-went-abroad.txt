The Dog Who Went Abroad

Spot, a dog from a town hit by a long famine, felt his throat constricted by hunger. He decided to leave his home and travel to another country in search of food.

In a new city, Spot found a careless housewife who left her food unattended. He entered her house every day and ate his fill from a variety of dishes. However, as he left, other dogs, fueled by arrogance, attacked him and tore him apart.

Spot realized that even in a foreign land, he faced danger and hardship. He thought, "It's better to return to my own city, where I can live peacefully, even during times of famine." So, he returned home.

When Spot's relatives asked him about his journey, he replied, "The food in the foreign land was delicious and varied. The people were easy-going, and the women were friendly. But the men were lacking in self-control."

Moral: Better one's own land, where one lives in peace, than a foreign place with good food and enemies.
