The Winning of Friends

In a city called Maidens' Delight, there was a giant banyan tree that sheltered many creatures. A crow named Swift lived in the tree and was known for his quick thinking.

One morning, Swift saw a hunter approaching the tree, armed with a snare and a club. The hunter looked frightening, with bloodshot eyes and a knot of hair on his head. Swift was curious about the hunter's intentions and decided to follow him.

As the hunter set up his snare, Swift warned the other birds to stay away, telling them that the rice-grains were poison. But a dove-king named Gay-Neck, with hundreds of doves, saw the food and ignored Swift's warning. He landed in the snare, and he and his doves were caught.

The hunter was delighted and started to lift his club. But Gay-Neck remained calm and came up with a plan. He told the doves to fly up and work together to carry the snare away. The doves were amazed by Gay-Neck's quick thinking and followed his instructions.

Together, the doves managed to lift the snare and fly away, leaving the hunter frustrated. But the snare was still tied around them. Gay-Neck led the doves to the city to find his friend, a mouse named Gold. Gold cut the bonds of every dove, and they were free to go home. Swift was impressed by Gay-Neck's leadership and teamwork skills. He realized that when friends work together, they can overcome even the most difficult challenges.

Moral: United action and teamwork can help overcome even the most daunting obstacles.
