How Supersmart Ate the Elephant

In a forest, a clever jackal named Supersmart lived. One day, he found an elephant that had died naturally. However, he couldn't cut through the elephant's tough hide.

Just then, a lion appeared. The jackal quickly rubbed his scalp in the dust, clasped his paws, and said, "My lord, I'm just a servant guarding this elephant for the king. Please, take it." The lion replied, "I don't eat what another has killed. I give it to you." The jackal said, "As the king's servant, I'm used to expecting this."

The lion left, and a tiger arrived. Supersmart thought, "I sent one away by flattering him. Now, how can I deal with this powerful tiger?" He remembered a saying: "Intrigue is the best way to get what you want." So, he went to meet the tiger and said, "Uncle, why did you come here? This elephant was killed by a lion, and he told me to warn him if any tiger came. He wants to get rid of all tigers because one helped himself to an elephant he had killed."

The tiger was frightened and begged for mercy, saying, "Please, nephew, don't tell the lion about me. I'll be in danger." With those words, the tiger ran away.

Next, a leopard appeared. Supersmart thought, "This leopard has powerful teeth. I'll use him to cut through the elephant's hide." He said, "Welcome, nephew! You're hungry, so you can eat this elephant meat. I'll warn you when the lion comes back." But the leopard replied, "I don't eat something that doesn't agree with me. I'll leave."

Supersmart said, "Don't be afraid. Eat, and I'll warn you when the lion is coming." The leopard did as suggested, and Supersmart called out, "Quick, nephew! The lion is coming!" The leopard fled, and Supersmart ate the meat through the opening.

Just then, another jackal appeared, and Supersmart fought him with his fangs, sending him away. He enjoyed the elephant meat for a long time.

Moral: A clever trickster can outsmart his enemies, but may not be able to outsmart his equals.
