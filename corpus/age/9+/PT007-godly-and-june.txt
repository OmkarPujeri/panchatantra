Godly and June

In a secluded monastery, a holy man named Godly lived. He had acquired a large sum of money by selling finely woven garments to the faithful. Godly trusted no one and kept his treasure close to him always.

A rogue named June, who stole money from others, observed Godly's treasure and thought: "How can I take this from him?" June couldn't break through the solid stone walls or enter the high door. He decided to win Godly's trust and become his disciple.

June approached Godly, bowed down, and said: "Glory to Shiva. Amen." He spoke with respect: "O holy sir, I see that life is short and fleeting. Delights are like a shadow. What should I do to safely cross the sea of life?" Godly was impressed by June's words.

Godly said: "You are blessed for being indifferent to the world at a young age. Saints are those who control their passions in youth. A holy sage's body may age, but their mind remains young." Godly then shared a prayer that could change one's caste and bring purity.

June listened and clasped Godly's feet. "Holy sir, please impose a vow on me." Godly agreed, but warned June not to enter his cell at night. June promised to follow the rules and take a vow.

As time passed, June served Godly, but Godly still did not trust him. June thought of killing Godly, but then a pupil's son arrived with an invitation for Godly to attend a ceremony. Godly took June along and, as they traveled, came to a river. He handed June his treasure and asked him to keep watch.

As soon as Godly was out of sight, June seized the treasure and ran away.
