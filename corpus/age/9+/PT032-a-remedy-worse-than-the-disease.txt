A Remedy Worse than the Disease

A flock of herons lived in a fig tree in a forest. A black snake lived in a hole and ate their chicks before they could fly.

One heron was so sad that he went to the pond and cried. A crab noticed him and asked, "What's wrong, Uncle?" The heron explained, "The snake in our tree is eating our babies. What can I do?"

The crab thought for a moment and said, "I'll give you a plan. Tell the other herons to put fish bits from the mungoose's burrow to the snake's hole. The mungoose will follow the trail and kill the snake."

The herons did as the crab suggested. The mungoose followed the fish bits and killed the snake. But then it ate all the herons who lived in the tree.

Moral: Sometimes, a solution to a problem can create even bigger problems.
