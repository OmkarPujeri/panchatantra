The Snake and the Ants

A massive black snake named Haughty lived in an ant-hill. He was known for his pride and arrogance.

One day, instead of following the beaten path out of his hole, Haughty tried to crawl through a narrower crevice. But his huge body got stuck, and he suffered a severe wound.

The ants, attracted by the smell of blood, swarmed around Haughty, biting and stinging him with their tiny but sharp jaws. Haughty tried to fight back, but the ants were relentless. He killed many ants, but they just kept coming, biting him in every spot.

Haughty's wounds grew bigger and bigger, and he became weaker and weaker. The ants continued to attack, and eventually, Haughty perished.

Moral: Even the strongest can fall when faced with the collective power of a united and angry crowd.
