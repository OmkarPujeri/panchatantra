The Foolish Friend

In a far-off kingdom, a merchant's son had befriended a prince, who made him his secretary of the treasury. Together, they hatched a plan to conquer a neighboring kingdom. The prince made the son of a man of learning his prime minister, and then became the king.

As time passed, the king grew lazy and indulged in luxurious pleasures. He made a pet of a mischievous monkey from the palace stables and appointed it his personal sword-bearer. The monkey was spoiled and grew big and strong.

One day, while the king and his queen were taking a stroll in a beautiful pleasure-grove, the monkey was left in charge. A bee buzzed around the king's head, and the monkey, thinking it was a threat, drew its sword and struck the king, killing him.

The queen was shocked and scolded the monkey, saying, "You fool! The king trusted you, and you repaid him with treachery."

The monkey explained what had happened, and the entire court shunned it. The king's death was a tragic reminder that making friends with foolish people can lead to disastrous consequences.

Moral: It's wiser to keep a safe distance from those who lack sense, for they can cause harm to others, just like the monkey killed the king.
