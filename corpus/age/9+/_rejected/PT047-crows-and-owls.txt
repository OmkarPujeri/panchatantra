Crows and Owls

In a southern city called Earth-Base, a great banyan tree stood tall. Cloudy, the crow-king, made his home in the tree with his countless retinue of crows. Nearby, in a mountain cave, lived Foe-Crusher, the owl-king, with his unnumbered retinue of owls. Foe-Crusher was a ruthless leader who killed any crow he encountered, spreading a circle of dead crows around the banyan tree.

Cloudy summoned his counselors and asked for their opinions on how to counter-attack Foe-Crusher. They proposed six possible courses of action: peace, war, change of base, entrenchment, alliances, and duplicity. Cloudy's first counselor, Live-Again, suggested making peace with Foe-Crusher, as it was often wise to bow to the great and powerful. However, the next counselor, Live-Well, disagreed, recommending war against the cruel and greedy owl-king.

The third counselor, Live-Along, proposed a change of base, suggesting that Cloudy retreat and then plan a counterattack. Live-On, the fourth counselor, argued against peace, war, and change of base, recommending instead that Cloudy entrench himself and prepare for battle. Live-Long, the fifth counselor, suggested forming an alliance with other birds to counter the owl-king's power.

After hearing these opinions, Cloudy turned to his wise and experienced counselor, Live-Strong, for guidance. Live-Strong revealed that he had been listening to all the proposals and had come to a different conclusion. He advised Cloudy to use duplicity, or cunning, to outwit Foe-Crusher. Live-Strong explained that by using spies and gathering information, Cloudy could discover a vulnerable point in the owl-king's camp and use it to their advantage.

Cloudy asked Live-Strong how he would discover Foe-Crusher's vulnerable point, and Live-Strong replied that he would use spies to gather the information. He explained that the king perceives through the use of spies, and that by knowing the functionaries and their character, Cloudy could gain the upper hand. Live-Strong described the functionaries in the owl-king's camp, including the counselor, chaplain, and commander-in-chief, and advised Cloudy to use secret-service men to gather information and sow intrigue among the enemy.

Cloudy asked Live-Strong about the origin of the deadly feud between crows and owls, and Live-Strong explained that it was a long-standing conflict between the two species. He told Cloudy that the key to success lay in using duplicity and cunning to outwit Foe-Crusher and his followers.

The story of Cloudy and his counselors serves as a reminder that sometimes the best course of action is not always clear-cut, and that using one's wits and cunning can be a powerful tool in achieving success.
