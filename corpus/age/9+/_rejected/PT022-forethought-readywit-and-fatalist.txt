Forethought, Readywit, and Fatalist

In a great lake lived three full-grown fishes. Forethought, Readywit, and Fatalist were their names. One day, Forethought overheard fishermen talking about fishing in the lake the next day. He thought, "This is bad news. They'll definitely come here tomorrow or the day after." So, he called Readywit and Fatalist.

Readywit said, "I've lived here for a long time. I won't move just because of rumors. If fishermen come, I'll think of a plan to protect myself." Fatalist said, "There are many lakes. Maybe they won't come here at all. We shouldn't leave our birthplace just because of gossip."

Forethought realized they wouldn't listen, so he left the lake. The next day, fishermen came with nets and caught all the fish. Readywit played dead to escape, but Fatalist got stuck in the net. The fishermen hit him with clubs, and he died.

A plover saw the scene and said, "My dear, why do you think like Fatalist?" She told them the story of Forethought and Readywit thriving while Fatalist died. She also told them not to worry about threats.

But the ocean, who had been listening, thought, "I'll test the plover's power." The next day, when the plover was out foraging, the ocean seized the eggs. The plover's wife returned to find their eggs gone and was devastated. She said, "I told you we should move, but you were like Fatalist, refusing to go. Now our children are gone, and I'll burn myself."

The plover said, "Wait, I'll show you my power. I'll dry up the ocean with my bill." But his wife said, "You can't fight the ocean. It's too powerful for you." The plover insisted, "I have a strong bill, and I'll dry up the ocean."

His wife said, "With a bill that holds one drop, you can't dry up the ocean. It's too big, and it has many tributary streams." The plover replied, "Success comes from determination. I have a strong will, and I'll make the ocean dry up." His wife said, "If you want to fight the ocean, at least call other birds to help you. A single weak bird can't defeat a strong enemy."

Moral: Forethought and planning can help you avoid danger, but determination and teamwork can help you overcome even the greatest challenges.
