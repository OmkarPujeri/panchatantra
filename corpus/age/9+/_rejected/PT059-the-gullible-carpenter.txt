The Gullible Carpenter

A carpenter in a village lived with his wife, who was known for her unfaithfulness.

He thought, "How can I test her? I've heard that even if a husband is far away, a wife's true nature remains the same." He decided to pretend to leave for another village and test her loyalty.

The next morning, he left his house, and his wife was overjoyed. She spent the day dressing up and laughing with her lover, who had come to the house while the carpenter was away.

That evening, the carpenter returned home, hiding under the bed. He saw his wife's lover in bed and was furious. He thought, "Should I attack him or wait until they fall asleep and kill them both?" But then he decided to observe and see how his wife behaved.

Just then, his wife stubbed her toe on the carpenter's body and thought, "It's him, the rascal, testing me!" She decided to trick him and told her lover, "I went to the goddess's shrine and was told to make you sleep with me to save my husband's life."

The lover was hesitant, but the wife convinced him to go along with the plan. The carpenter, still hiding under the bed, heard the whole conversation and thought his wife was being faithful.

When the wife and her lover finished their act, the carpenter emerged from under the bed, hugged his wife, and said, "Bravo, faithful wife! I was testing you, and you passed the test!" He then forced the lover to mount his shoulder and danced around the village, celebrating his supposed good fortune.

Moral: Trusting someone's words without evidence can lead to serious mistakes.
