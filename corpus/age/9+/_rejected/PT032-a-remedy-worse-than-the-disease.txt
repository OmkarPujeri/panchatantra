A Remedy Worse than the Disease

A group of herons lived in a forest, nesting on a fig tree. A black snake lived in a hole in the tree, eating heron chicks before they could fly. One heron, heartbroken over the loss of her babies, went to the pond and cried. A crab asked, "Uncle, why are you so sad?" The heron explained, "The snake in the fig tree is eating my chicks and those of my relatives. I'm desperate for a solution."

The crab thought, "The snake is a natural enemy of my kind, so I'll give the heron a false plan that will harm both the herons and the snake." He said, "Uncle, scatter fish bits from the mungoose's burrow to the snake's hole. The mungoose will follow the trail and kill the snake." The heron did as advised, and the mungoose followed the fish bits, killing the snake and eating all the herons who lived in the tree.

Moral: A well-intentioned plan can sometimes lead to more harm than good.
