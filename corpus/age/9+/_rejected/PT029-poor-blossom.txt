Poor Blossom

Indra, the king of gods, had a beautiful parrot named Blossom. The parrot was incredibly smart and had learned many scientific formulas.

One day, Blossom was resting on Indra's palm, enjoying the warmth and comfort. He started reciting his favorite formulas when he spotted Yama, the god of death, approaching. Blossom quickly moved away, and the other gods asked him, "Why did you move away from Yama?" Blossom replied, "He brings harm to all living creatures. Why should I stay near him?"

The gods tried to reassure Blossom, saying, "Don't worry, we'll ask Yama not to harm you." Yama replied, "I'm just doing my job. It's not up to me." The gods then took Blossom to meet Time, who said, "Death is in charge of this matter. Ask him."

The gods asked Death, but at the mere sight of him, Blossom died. The gods were shocked and asked Yama, "Why did this happen?" Yama replied, "It was simply fate that Blossom would die when he saw Death."

Moral: Sometimes, no matter how hard we try, fate can be unstoppable.
