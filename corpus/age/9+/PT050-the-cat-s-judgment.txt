The Cat's Judgment

I lived in a tree, and beneath it, a partridge made his home. We became great friends, sharing stories, jokes, and adventures. One evening, the partridge failed to return from foraging, and I worried about his safety. Many days passed, and I missed him dearly.

A rabbit named Speedy moved into the partridge's nest, and I didn't object, thinking the partridge was gone for good. But one day, the partridge returned, plump and proud from eating rice. He was furious to find Speedy in his nest and demanded he leave.

Speedy argued, "A dwelling belongs to whoever occupies it." The partridge countered, "Let's ask the neighbors for their testimony." Speedy retorted, "You must know the tradition that says a neighbor's testimony is decisive after ten years of living together."

The partridge proposed, "If you're right, let's consult the specialists. They'll decide who the nest belongs to." Speedy agreed, and together they set off to find a judge. I followed, curious about the outcome.

As they walked, Speedy asked, "Who will pass judgment on our dispute?" The partridge replied, "There's a wise tomcat named Curd-Ear who lives on a sand-bank by the Ganges River. He's a saint, known for his compassion."

Speedy's eyes widened in terror as he spotted the cat. "No, no! He's a scoundrel. You can't trust him." But I noticed that the cat seemed genuinely concerned and began to speak in a moralizing tone.

The partridge and Speedy asked the cat to judge their dispute, with a condition: if either of them spoke falsely, the cat could eat them. The cat, however, protested, "I abhor cruelty. Why would I hurt innocent creatures?" He then launched into a long, pious speech about the importance of kindness and compassion.

The partridge and Speedy, charmed by the cat's words, drew near to explain their case. The cat, feigning old age and difficulty hearing, invited them to come closer. As soon as they did, he pounced, catching both the partridge and Speedy in his paws and teeth. He ate them both, proving that a corrupt judge can be a deadly trap.

Moral: A biased or corrupt judge can lead to devastating consequences, and fairness and integrity are essential for making just decisions.
