The Fiend Who Washed His Feet

In a dense forest, a fiend named Cruel lived. 
He met a Brahman and climbed onto his shoulder. 
"Let's go," said Cruel.

The terrified Brahman started walking with Cruel on his back. 
Cruel's soft feet caught the Brahman's attention. 
"Why are your feet so soft?" asked the Brahman.

Cruel replied, "I'm under a vow to wash my feet before touching the ground." 
The Brahman thought, "This is my chance to escape." 
He spotted a lake nearby and had an idea.

The Brahman said, "Wait here, I'll stay put until you return from bathing." 
Cruel agreed and entered the lake. 
The Brahman quickly ran away, thinking, "He won't follow me with unwashed feet."

But Cruel, bound by his vow, didn't dare to chase the Brahman. 
He stayed in the lake, unable to break his promise. 
The Brahman escaped safely, and Cruel was left behind.

Moral: A prudent man should always think ahead and not be fooled by appearances.
