The Ass in the Tiger-Skin

A poor laundryman lived in a village with his old donkey.

His donkey was weak and hungry, and the laundryman wanted to feed it.

One day, while the laundryman was gathering firewood, he found a dead tiger.

He thought, "This is a lucky find. I can use the tiger-skin to trick the farmers."

He put the tiger-skin on the donkey and let it loose in the barley fields at night.

The donkey ate as much barley as it wanted, and at dawn, the laundryman brought it back to the barn.

The donkey grew fat and lazy, and soon it couldn't even fit into its stall.

But one day, it heard another donkey's bray in the distance, and it started braying too.

The farmers saw through the disguise and chased the donkey with clubs, stones, and arrows.

The donkey was killed, and the laundryman was left with nothing.

Moral: Even the cleverest disguises can't fool everyone forever.
