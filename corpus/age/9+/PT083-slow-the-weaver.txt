Slow, the Weaver

In a certain town lived a weaver named Slow. One day, his loom's pegs broke. He took an axe to find wood and came to the seashore. There, he found a great sissoo tree. Slow thought, "This tree is perfect. I can make many weaving tools if I cut it down." He lifted his axe.

A fairy lived in the tree, saying, "Please spare this tree. I live here happily, surrounded by cool breezes from the ocean." Slow replied, "I need wood for my tools. My family is hungry. Please move elsewhere." The fairy said, "I like you. Ask for anything, but spare this tree."

Slow thought, "I'll go home and ask my friend and wife." The fairy agreed, and Slow left. In the town, he met his friend, the barber. "My dear friend," Slow said, "I've won the fairy's favor. What should I ask for?" The barber replied, "Ask for a kingdom. You'll be king, and I'll be your prime minister."

Slow thought, "That's a good idea." But he went home to ask his wife. "Don't," the barber said. "Women are not wise. They think only of their pleasures." Slow ignored the barber's warning and asked his wife, "What should I ask for?" She replied, "You turn out one piece of cloth a day, which meets our expenses. Ask for a second pair of arms and an extra head, so you can produce two pieces of cloth."

Slow was delighted and asked the fairy for his wish. The fairy granted it, and Slow became two-headed and four-armed. However, the people thought he was a monster and beat him with clubs and stones, killing him.

Moral: Lacking wisdom, one may not see the consequences of one's actions, leading to harm.
