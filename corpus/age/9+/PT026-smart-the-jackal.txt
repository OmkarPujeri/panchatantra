Smart, the Jackal

In a forest, a lion named Thunder-Fang lived with his advisors: Meat-Face the wolf, Smart the jackal, and Spike-Ear the camel. One day, Thunder-Fang fought a fierce elephant and was badly hurt. He ordered his famished friends to find food, but they couldn't find anything.

Smart thought, "If I can trick the lion into killing Spike-Ear, we'll have food for a few days." He approached Spike-Ear and said, "Friend, the lion is starving and if he leaves, we'll all starve too. I have a plan to save us both. If the lion kills you, we'll have food for a while, and he'll live longer too." Spike-Ear agreed, thinking it was a noble deed.

Smart proposed that Spike-Ear give his body to the lion at 100% interest, promising a double body in return. Spike-Ear agreed to this, but asked the Death-God to guarantee the bargain. The lion agreed, and Spike-Ear was killed.

When the lion went to the river to bathe, Smart said to Meat-Face, "You're starving, so eat the camel's heart before the lion returns." Meat-Face took a bite, but Smart shouted, "Drop it! The lion is coming!" When the lion returned, he saw the camel's heart was missing and was furious.

Smart blamed Meat-Face for the missing heart, so Meat-Face fled for his life. The lion chased him a short way, then turned back. Just then, a camel caravan was heard in the distance. The lion asked Smart to find out what the noise was. Smart pretended to investigate, then shouted, "Run, master! The Death-God is coming to punish you for killing Spike-Ear and eating the camel's heart!"

The lion fled, leaving Smart to eat the camel's body. Smart ate the meat slowly, making it last a long time.

Moral: Those who wrong others for their own good should keep their plans a secret, as Smart did.
