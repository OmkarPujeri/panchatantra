The Self-sacrificing Dove

In a forest, a ruthless fowler plied his trade,
Killing birds for a living, leaving none behind.

He had no friends, no family, only his cruel ways.
All creatures feared him, and he was left to his lonely days.

One day, a fierce storm rolled in, and the fowler sought shelter.
He stumbled upon a tree, where a dove's nest was nestled.

The dove, a devoted wife, was grieving for her absent mate.
She sang a sorrowful song, missing her husband's loving presence.

The fowler, caught in the dove's song, was moved by her devotion.
He asked the dove if she loved her husband more than life itself.

The dove replied, "A wife's love is not just for her husband's needs,
But for his happiness, and for the home they build together."

The dove then told the fowler, "A true guest is one who is in need,
And it is our duty to help those who seek shelter and aid."

The fowler, touched by the dove's words, felt a pang of guilt.
He realized that his ways were wrong, and that he had been living in sin.

The dove, seeing the fowler's change of heart, offered him food and warmth.
But the fowler, consumed by his own desires, refused the dove's kindness.

The dove, sacrificing himself, took pity on the fowler's plight.
He gave his life to save the fowler, and as he died, he ascended to heaven.

The dove's wife, upon seeing her husband's sacrifice, was filled with sorrow.
She realized that her love for her husband was stronger than life itself.

In a flash of divine insight, she followed her husband into the fire,
And was transformed into a radiant being, shining with heavenly light.

The fowler, overcome with grief and regret, entered the forest-fire,
And was cleansed of his sins, rising to heaven, free from his wicked ways.

Moral: Sacrificing oneself for the greater good is the ultimate form of devotion.
