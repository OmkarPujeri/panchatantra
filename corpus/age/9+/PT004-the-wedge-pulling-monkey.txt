The Wedge-pulling Monkey

In a city, a merchant was building a temple. Workers went to the city for lunch at noon.

A troop of monkeys came upon the half-built temple. A craftsman had begun to split a huge log, and he had driven a wedge into the crack at the top.

The monkeys began to play on the treetops, the roof, and the woodpile. Then one monkey, whose doom was near, climbed onto the log without thinking. "Who stuck a wedge in this strange place?" he wondered.

He seized the wedge with both hands and worked it loose. When the wedge slipped free, the split log snapped shut on him, and he was crushed.

And that is why the wise say that meddling should be avoided.

Moral: Meddling brings ruin on the meddler.
