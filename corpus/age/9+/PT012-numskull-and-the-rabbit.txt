Numskull and the Rabbit

In a forest, a lion named Numskull was notorious for his cruel ways. He would slaughter animals without mercy.

The other animals, including deer, boars, buffaloes, wild oxen, rabbits, and many more, came together to plead with Numskull to stop his brutal behavior. They explained that his actions would lead to a miserable afterlife.

Numskull listened to their words, but his pride wouldn't let him change. He threatened to kill all the animals if one didn't come to him as his daily meal.

One day, a rabbit's turn came. He was late, but Numskull was hungrier than usual. The rabbit explained that he had been sent by the other animals, as per their agreement, to be Numskull's meal.

Numskull's anger grew, but the rabbit calmly told him that he had been held up by a lion who claimed the forest as his own. This lion had challenged Numskull to a fight, and the rabbit was sent to bring Numskull to the lion.

Intrigued, Numskull decided to go with the rabbit. They arrived at a well, where the rabbit showed Numskull the lion. But instead of seeing his rival, Numskull saw his own reflection in the water.

The lion, thinking he was facing a powerful opponent, let out a loud roar. But the echo from the well made it sound twice as loud, and the lion, thinking he was being attacked, hurled himself down and met his death.

The rabbit returned to the other animals, telling them the good news. They praised him for his cleverness and thanked him for saving them from Numskull's cruel wrath.

Moral: Intelligence and clever thinking can often lead to success, even when facing seemingly insurmountable challenges.
