How the Rabbit Fooled the Elephant

In a forest lived an elephant-king named Four-Tusk, who protected his herd. A severe drought struck, and the elephants begged for a solution. Four-Tusk sent eight groups to search for water in different directions.

One group discovered the Lake of the Moon, a beautiful lake with swans, herons, and other water creatures. The lake was surrounded by flowering trees and had a gentle slope, perfect for elephants to bathe. However, the elephants didn't notice the rabbits living on the banks.

The rabbits, who had lost many of their family members, held an emergency meeting. They asked Victory, a wise rabbit, to come up with a plan to prevent the elephants from returning. Victory promised to stop the elephants and said he had received a special gift from his guardian angel.

The rabbit-king, Block-Snout, trusted Victory and sent him to meet the elephant-king. Victory climbed a rock-pile and introduced himself as an envoy from the Moon. The elephant-king, Four-Tusk, was impressed by Victory's confidence and asked him to state his business.

Victory explained that as an envoy, he was protected by the laws of kings. He told Four-Tusk that he had sinned against the Moon by killing the rabbits who were under its protection. Four-Tusk felt guilty and asked Victory to show him the way to win the Moon's forgiveness.

Victory led Four-Tusk to the Lake of the Moon at night and showed him the reflection of the Moon in the water. Four-Tusk was amazed by the beauty of the Moon and its companions. He felt ashamed and decided to purify himself by washing his trunk in the lake.

However, Victory was shocked to see Four-Tusk touch the water, which he knew would anger the Moon even more. Four-Tusk realized his mistake and bowed his head in deep obeisance, asking for forgiveness. He begged the Moon to forgive him and promised never to return. Then he went back to his own place, and the elephants never troubled the rabbits again.

Moral: A clever trick can save you from a foe much stronger than you.
