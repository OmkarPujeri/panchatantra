The Jackal and the War-Drum

In a forest, a hungry jackal wandered, searching for food. He stumbled upon a king's battle ground, where he heard a loud drumming sound.

The jackal's heart sank, thinking, "Disaster is upon me! I'm doomed!" He wondered who made the sound and what kind of creature it was.

Curious, he peeked around and spotted a massive war-drum. He thought, "Was that sound its natural voice or was it made by someone else?" The drum only made a sound when the wind swayed the grasses against it, but was silent at other times.

The jackal crept closer, seeing the drum's helplessness. His curiosity got the better of him, and he struck the drum on both heads, feeling proud. "Aha! Food has finally come to me! This drum must be stuffed with meat and fat!"

Excited, he picked a spot, gnawed a hole, and crawled inside. Though the leather was tough, he managed to squeeze in without breaking his teeth. But to his disappointment, he found only wood and skin inside.

The jackal backed out, chuckling to himself. "I thought it was full of fat, but all I found was wood and skin." He realized his foolish mistake and slunk away, still hungry.

Moral: Be careful not to jump to conclusions, or you might end up with a hollow victory.
