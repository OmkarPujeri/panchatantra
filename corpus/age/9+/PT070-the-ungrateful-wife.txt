The Ungrateful Wife

There was a Brahman who loved his wife more than his life. However, she was always quarreling with his family and never stopped complaining.

One day, the Brahman left his family and started a new life in another country, far away from the forest where they lived. His wife asked him to find some water, but when he returned, he found her dead.

The Brahman was heartbroken, but he heard a voice from heaven saying, "Brahman, give half your life, and your wife will live." He performed a purification ceremony and repeated the magic words, "I give life." His wife sat up, alive.

Together, they drank water and ate forest fruits, and then the Brahman left his wife in a flower garden near a city. As he sang a song, a cripple turned towards him, and his wife fell in love with him.

She asked the cripple to be her husband, and he agreed. When the Brahman returned with food, his wife asked him to give the cripple a bite. Then she suggested taking the cripple with them, and the Brahman agreed, thinking it was a good idea.

However, the wife and the cripple had a different plan. They pushed the Brahman into a well, and the wife took the cripple to the city. The king saw the cripple and took the basket from the wife, who then told the king that the cripple was her invalid husband.

The king believed her and gave her two villages to live in with her husband. But the Brahman arrived in the city, and the wife accused him of being her enemy. The king sentenced him to death.

The Brahman asked the king to make his wife return what she had taken from him. The wife denied taking anything, but the Brahman reminded her of the magic words she had spoken earlier. She repeated the words, "I give life," and fell dead.

The king was amazed and asked the Brahman to explain what had happened. The Brahman told him the whole story.

Moral: A person's true nature is revealed when they are given power and wealth.
