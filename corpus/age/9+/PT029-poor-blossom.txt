Poor Blossom

Adapted from the Panchatantra

God Indra had a parrot named Blossom. He was incredibly beautiful, intelligent, and well-learned. Blossom loved to sit on Indra's hand, feeling happy and content.

One day, while Blossom was resting on Indra's hand, he saw Yama, the god of death, approaching. Blossom immediately moved away, and the other gods asked him, "Why did you move away, Blossom?" Blossom replied, "Yama brings harm to all living creatures. Why shouldn't I move away from him?"

The gods tried to calm Blossom's fears by asking Yama to spare his life. Yama said, "I don't decide these things. It's Time who decides when someone dies." The gods then took Blossom to meet Time, asking him to spare the parrot's life. But Time said, "It's Death who makes these decisions. Ask him."

The gods spoke to Death and asked him to spare Blossom. But Blossom died at the mere sight of Death. The gods were shocked and asked Yama, "Why did this happen?" Yama replied, "Blossom's death was simply his fate."

With that, the gods returned to heaven.

Moral: Sometimes, no matter how hard we try, our fate is already decided.
