The Lion and the Ram

In a forest, a lone ram wandered, his thick fleece and sharp horns making him look fierce.

A lion, who ruled over many animals, saw the ram and felt a surge of fear. "He's too powerful," the lion thought. "That's why he roams so boldly." The lion backed away, not wanting to fight.

But the lion returned, curious about the ram's strength. He watched as the ram peacefully grazed on the forest floor. "He's a gentle creature," the lion thought. "If he eats grass, he must be weak." With a swift pounce, the lion killed the ram.

The lion realized too late that he had misjudged the ram. He had let fear and misunderstanding lead him to make a terrible mistake.
