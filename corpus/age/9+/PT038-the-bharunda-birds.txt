The Bharunda Birds

In a beautiful lake, there lived birds with a unique feature - one belly and two necks each.

One sunny day, a bharunda bird strolled around, and its first neck spotted some sweet nectar. The second neck saw it and exclaimed, "Give me half!" But the first neck refused, saying it had found it first.

The second neck, feeling angry and entitled, went off to find something to punish its companion. It picked up some poisonous food and ate it. Since they shared the same belly, both necks suffered and eventually died.

Moral: Greed and selfishness can lead to disaster, even when you think you're right.
