The Unteachable Monkey

In a forest, a troop of monkeys were shivering with cold one winter evening. They spotted a firefly and thought it was a tiny flame. With great care, they lifted the firefly, covered it with dry grass and leaves, and surrounded themselves with it. They even blew on it, imagining the warmth spreading through their bodies.

The monkey who was the chilliest of all blew repeatedly, his breath hot with excitement. A bird named Needle-Face, who had been watching from a nearby tree, flew down to warn the monkey. "This isn't fire, it's just a firefly!" she cried, but the monkey wouldn't listen.

Needle-Face tried to reason with the monkey, but he just kept blowing, ignoring her warnings. When she shouted right into his ear, he got angry and grabbed her, slamming her against a rock. Unfortunately, the bird's head and neck were badly hurt, and she didn't survive.

The monkey did not learn from what happened. He would not listen, even when the bird tried again and again. An unfit mind cannot be taught, no matter how good the advice.

Moral: Blind stubbornness can lead to great harm, and it's wise to listen to others' advice.
