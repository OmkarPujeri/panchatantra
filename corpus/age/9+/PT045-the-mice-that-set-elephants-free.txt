The Mice That Set Elephants Free

There was once a region where homes and temples had fallen into disrepair. Mice had made their homes in the cracks of the floors of grand houses, raising families and living in comfort. They enjoyed many festivals and performances, and their lives were filled with joy.

But one day, an elephant-king and his herd of thousands marched through the area, searching for a lake. As they passed through the mouse community, they crushed many mice beneath their feet.

The surviving mice gathered to discuss their problem. "We're being killed by these massive elephants," they said. "If they come back, we'll be extinct. We need a plan to stop them."

After some thought, a group of mice came up with an idea. They traveled to the lake, where the elephant-king was resting, and respectfully asked him to change his route. "We've lived here for generations," they said. "If you continue to pass through our homes, we'll be destroyed. Please consider our plea and find a new path."

The elephant-king listened to their words and decided that they made sense. He agreed to change his route, and the mice were saved.

Later, the elephant-king found himself trapped in a water-trap set by a king's elephant-trappers. He thought, "I have no way to escape except with the help of those clever mice."

The elephant-king sent a message to the mice through one of his elephants, describing his situation. The mice quickly gathered and set out to rescue their friends. They gnawed through the ropes holding the elephant-king and his herd captive, then climbed the trees to cut the ropes above. With the ropes severed, the elephants were free.

Moral: Make friends, no matter how strong or powerful you are, and they will help you in times of need.
