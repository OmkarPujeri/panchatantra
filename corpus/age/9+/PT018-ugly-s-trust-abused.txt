Ugly's Trust Abused

In a distant city, a merchant named Ocean loaded a hundred camels with valuable cloth and set out on a journey. One of his camels, Ugly, was overburdened and fell limp, so Ocean divided the pack and left Ugly behind.

Days passed, and Ugly regained his strength by eating grass in the forest. A lion named Haughty, who lived in the forest with a leopard, a crow, and a jackal, came across Ugly. Haughty was impressed by Ugly's story and guaranteed his safety.

However, Haughty fought an elephant one day and was wounded by a tusk. He had to keep to his cave. After five or six days, all of them were in distress from the lack of food. Haughty told them he could not supply the usual food while wounded, and urged them to hunt up a creature for them both to eat.

The jackal and the crow started to plot against Ugly, suggesting that he be killed to feed Haughty. The jackal went to Haughty and proposed that Ugly be sacrificed, but Haughty was hesitant, remembering his promise to protect Ugly.

The jackal convinced Haughty that Ugly would volunteer to be killed if he knew it was for Haughty's sake. The other three animals then offered to sacrifice themselves, one by one, to feed Haughty. Each of them gave eloquent speeches, pleading with Haughty to accept their offer.

Ugly, however, thought that the others were using fancy words to avoid being eaten. He decided to make a speech of his own, hoping to outdo the others. Ugly bowed low and said, "Master, you can't eat any of them. Please take my life instead, so I can win the best of earth and heaven."

Haughty, moved by Ugly's words, gave the order, and the others tore Ugly's body apart. They devoured him, and Ugly finally gave up the ghost.

Moral: Those who live by their wits often end up being devoured by their own cleverness.
