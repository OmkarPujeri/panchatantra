# Certification pass — 2026-10-13

**Purpose.** Census 1 flagged 23 tellings and cleared 119. I verified all 23 but never verified
the 119 it *cleared*. This pass re-audited those 119, adversarially, to find what census 1 excused.

**Result: not clean.** 45 refutations across 42 story-band pairs. The corpus could not be
certified.

## Method

30 read-only agents, batches of 4, each told: *"a first census marked these RESOLVES — your job is
to REFUTE that."* They checked a **broader** defect set than census 1, which only looked for
invented/truncated endings:

| verdict | count |
|---|---|
| ACTOR_MISASSIGNED | 14 |
| INVENTED | 12 |
| PLOT_CONTRADICTS | 7 |
| MORAL_UNEARNED | 7 |
| TRUNCATED | 5 |

**Why this is a real signal, not noise.** I told these agents to *"default to flagging when
uncertain"*, which biases toward false positives — so I spot-verified the three sharpest claims
before believing any of it, and **all three held**:

- **PT002 9+** — the telling puts *"It will take twelve years"* in **Keen's** mouth as his clever
  idea. The source gives that doctrine to the advisors collectively (line 40) and gives Keen the
  **opposite** proposal (line 42). His entire rhetorical role is to reject the long timeline.
  The 4-9 band has it right.
- **PT005 4-9** — *"the drum was quiet when the wind blew."* Source: *"when the drum was struck by
  the tips of grasses swaying in the wind, it made the sound."* Inverted.
- **PT006 9+** — *"Bull was overjoyed and apologized for his actions."* Source line 156: Bull, the
  wrongdoer, says *"Friend merchant, I forgive you."* The relationship is reversed.

## What this means

Census 1 was **not wrong, it was narrow.** It asked "does the telling resolve?", answered that
correctly, and never asked "is the telling's *plot* right?" A telling can resolve perfectly and
still invert who did what to whom.

So the honest status is **not** "certified clean". It is: 23 resolution defects found and fixed,
then 42 further content defects found on a second, broader pass — unverified in bulk and unfixed.
